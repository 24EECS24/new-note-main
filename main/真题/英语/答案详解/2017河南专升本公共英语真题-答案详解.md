## Part I Vocabulary and Structure（词汇与结构，40分，40×1）

**Directions:** There are 40 incomplete sentences in this part. For each sentence there are four choices marked A, B, C and D. Choose the ONE that best completes the sentence, and then mark the corresponding letter on the Answer Sheet.

**1.** In order to search for the escaped prisoner, the police decide to question ____ comes along this road.（==C==）

- A. who
- B. whom
- C. whoever
- D. whomever

> [!note]- 解析
> question 后接宾语从句，从句中作主语、意为“任何……的人”，用 whoever = anyone who。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词性从句（whoever）。

**2.** Albert Einstein often stressed to interviewers that his accomplishments would certainly have been achieved by others, ____ lived.（==B==）

- A. he had never
- B. never had he
- C. never he had
- D. had he never

> [!note]- 解析
> 否定副词 never 置于句首，主句部分倒装：never had he (lived)。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 否定副词倒装。

**3.** Some companies have introduced flexible working time with less emphasis on pressure ____.（==A==）

- A. and more on efficiency
- B. and more efficiency
- C. than efficiency
- D. than more on efficiency

> [!note]- 解析
> less emphasis on pressure 与 more emphasis on efficiency 平行比较，承前省略 emphasis。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 平行比较结构。

**4.** They are going to have the serviceman ____ an air-conditioner in the office next week.（==A==）

- A. install
- B. to install
- C. to be installed
- D. installed

> [!note]- 解析
> have sb. do sth.（让某人做某事），serviceman 与 install 为主动。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> have sb. do。

**5.** More and more people begin to realize that it is about time the government ____ further measures to control pollution.（==D==）

- A. must take
- B. is taking
- C. takes
- D. took

> [!note]- 解析
> It is about/high time that… 从句用一般过去时（或 should + 动词原形）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> It is about time 虚拟。

**6.** While crossing the mountain areas, all the men had guns for protection lest they ____ by the local bandits.（==A==）

- A. be attacked
- B. were attacked
- C. must be attacked
- D. would be attacked

> [!note]- 解析
> lest（以免）后用 (should) + 动词原形，they 与 attack 为被动，故 be attacked。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> lest 虚拟语气（被动）。

**7.** The manager promised to keep me ____ of how our business was going on.（==C==）

- A. to be informed
- B. on informing
- C. informed
- D. informing

> [!note]- 解析
> keep sb. informed of（随时告知某人），过去分词作宾补。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> keep + 宾补。

**8.** ____ mirror the ideas and opinions of women in the United States in the later part of the 1800s.（==B==）

- A. Whether the novels of Mary Jane Homes
- B. The novels by Mary Jane Homes
- C. Mary Jane Homes wrote novels
- D. Why Mary Jane Homes wrote novels

> [!note]- 解析
> 句子缺主语与谓语 mirror，只有 The novels…（主语）+ mirror（谓语）结构完整。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 句子结构（主谓）。

**9.** ____ my father wants to do after he retires is to write a book.（==C==）

- A. Which
- B. That
- C. What
- D. Those

> [!note]- 解析
> 主语从句中 do 缺宾语，“……的事情”用 what。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 主语从句连接词。

**10.** The speed ____ light travels is said to be the maximum in the universe.（==D==）

- A. by which
- B. from which
- C. in which
- D. at which

> [!note]- 解析
> at the speed of（以……速度），定语从句介词提前用 at which。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 定语从句（介词+which）。

**11.** Steven ____ my letter; otherwise he would have replied before now.（==C==）

- A. has received
- B. should have received
- C. couldn't have received
- D. ought to have received

> [!note]- 解析
> otherwise 含蓄虚拟：“否则他早回信了”，推断他不可能收到信，用 couldn't have done。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 含蓄虚拟/情态动词推测。

**12.** Your hair wants ____. You'd better have it done tomorrow.（==B==）

- A. to cut
- B. cutting
- C. cut
- D. being cut

> [!note]- 解析
> want doing = want to be done（需要被……），主动形式表被动。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> want doing 主动表被动。

**13.** Tom can't speak Chinese, but Peter ____.（==B==）

- A. do
- B. does
- C. did
- D. done

> [!note]- 解析
> 用 does 代替 speaks（一般现在时，第三人称单数）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 替代词 do。

**14.** All those are matters ____ attention.（==C==）

- A. is worthy
- B. worthy
- C. worthy of
- D. which is worthy of

> [!note]- 解析
> be worthy of（值得），worthy of attention 作后置定语修饰 matters。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> worthy of 搭配。

**15.** But for your help, I ____.（==D==）

- A. can have failed again
- B. may have failed again
- C. will have failed again
- D. would have failed again

> [!note]- 解析
> but for（要不是）含蓄条件，对过去虚拟主句用 would have done。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> but for 含蓄虚拟。

**16.** Mr. Grant ____ in Hangzhou since 2006. He loves this city.（==A==）

- A. has been living
- B. lived
- C. will be living
- D. will have been living

> [!note]- 解析
> since 2006 与“一直住”呼应，用现在完成进行时 has been living。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 现在完成进行时。

**17.** They were in ____ when they left that they forgot the airline tickets.（==C==）

- A. a rush so anxious
- B. a such anxious rush
- C. such an anxious rush
- D. so an anxious rush

> [!note]- 解析
> such + a/an + adj. + 单数名词 + that 从句；so + adj. + a/an + 名词。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> such…that 结构。

**18.** Under no circumstances ____ our car.（==B==）

- A. they are borrow
- B. are they to borrow
- C. they will borrow
- D. could they be borrowing

> [!note]- 解析
> Under no circumstances（绝不）置于句首，部分倒装，are they to borrow。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 否定状语倒装。

**19.** You're going to the United States next year. You should now practice ____ English as much as possible.（==C==）

- A. speak
- B. to speak
- C. speaking
- D. spoke

> [!note]- 解析
> practice doing sth.（练习做某事）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> practice doing。

**20.** All flights ____ because of the snowstorm, we decided to take the train.（==D==）

- A. were canceled
- B. had been canceled
- C. having canceled
- D. having been canceled

> [!note]- 解析
> 独立主格：flights 与 cancel 为被动且先于 decided，用现在分词完成被动式 having been canceled。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 独立主格结构。

**21.** Only one little boy ____ the accident; everyone else was killed.（==A==）

- A. survived
- B. submitted
- C. suffered
- D. succeeded

> [!note]- 解析
> survive（幸免于）；submit 服从；suffer 遭受；succeed 成功。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**22.** I don't know whether what he said is true, but I'll try to ____ it.（==D==）

- A. confine
- B. confess
- C. conform
- D. confirm

> [!note]- 解析
> confirm（证实、确认）；confine 限制；confess 承认；conform 遵守。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词形近词辨析。

**23.** My camera can be ____ to take pictures in cloudy or sunny conditions.（==B==）

- A. treated
- B. adjusted
- C. adopted
- D. remedied

> [!note]- 解析
> adjust（调节、调整）；adopt 采纳；remedy 补救。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**24.** Children who are over-protected by their parents may become ____.（==C==）

- A. hurt
- B. damaged
- C. spoiled
- D. harmed

> [!note]- 解析
> spoiled（被宠坏的）；over-protected 与 spoiled 呼应。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词/形容词词义。

**25.** I think the whole paper has to be rewritten; only the first few paragraphs may be ____.（==C==）

- A. sustained
- B. contained
- C. retained
- D. maintained

> [!note]- 解析
> retain（保留）；sustain 维持；contain 包含；maintain 维护。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**26.** The boat ____, throwing the boys into the water.（==D==）

- A. turned on
- B. turned down
- C. turned off
- D. turned over

> [!note]- 解析
> turn over（翻船、倾覆）；turn on 打开；turn down 拒绝；turn off 关闭。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语动词。

**27.** The teacher asked a difficult question, but finally Peter ____ a good answer.（==C==）

- A. came up to
- B. came up for
- C. came up with
- D. came up against

> [!note]- 解析
> come up with（想出、提出）；come up to 达到；come up against 遇到。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语动词。

**28.** Mr. Wang is getting too old for football. He decides to ____ tennis instead.（==B==）

- A. take in
- B. take up
- C. take for
- D. take over

> [!note]- 解析
> take up（开始从事）；take in 吸收；take over 接管。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语动词。

**29.** Not long ago, a person who I know very well was ____ an accident.（==C==）

- A. related to
- B. included in
- C. involved in
- D. subjected to

> [!note]- 解析
> be involved in（卷入、参与）；related to 与……有关；subjected to 使遭受。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语辨析。

**30.** It is our ____ policy that we will achieve unity through peaceful means.（==A==）

- A. consistent
- B. considerate
- C. continual
- D. continuous

> [!note]- 解析
> consistent（一贯的、始终如一的）；considerate 体贴的；continual/continuous 连续的。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词词义。

**31.** Many people complain of the rapid ____ of modern life.（==B==）

- A. rate
- B. pace
- C. speed
- D. growth

> [!note]- 解析
> the rapid pace of modern life（现代生活快节奏），pace 指生活节奏。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词搭配。

**32.** We discussed this problem for several days, but couldn't come to a(n) ____.（==C==）

- A. result
- B. end
- C. conclusion
- D. judgment

> [!note]- 解析
> come to a conclusion（得出结论）为固定搭配。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> come to a conclusion。

**33.** I am ____ every day by the slow bus service in this city.（==A==）

- A. annoyed
- B. confused
- C. horrified
- D. irritated

> [!note]- 解析
> be annoyed by（被……惹恼）；irritated 也可，但常用搭配 be annoyed by。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义/搭配。

**34.** No one likes him because his action doesn't ____ his words.（==A==）

- A. correspond with
- B. agree on
- C. communicate with
- D. consist of

> [!note]- 解析
> correspond with（与……一致、相符）；agree on 就……达成一致；consist of 由……组成。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语动词。

**35.** His knowledge of English literature is ____ mine.（==B==）

- A. superior than
- B. superior to
- C. superior more than
- D. superior better to

> [!note]- 解析
> be superior to（优于），to 为介词，不用 than。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> superior to 搭配。

**36.** He rarely goes to the cinema as his work ____ almost all his time.（==C==）

- A. takes away
- B. takes over
- C. takes up
- D. takes in

> [!note]- 解析
> take up（占据时间）；take away 拿走；take over 接管；take in 吸收。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语动词。

**37.** The World-Cup football match will be televised ____ to the world.（==C==）

- A. alive
- B. living
- C. live
- D. lively

> [!note]- 解析
> live（现场直播地）；alive 活着的；lively 活泼的。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形近副词辨析。

**38.** The restaurant is busy on Sundays, so I'll phone up today and ____ a table.（==B==）

- A. preserve
- B. reserve
- C. observe
- D. deserve

> [!note]- 解析
> reserve a table（预订桌位）；preserve 保存；observe 观察；deserve 值得。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**39.** Never waste anything, but ____ never waste time.（==C==）

- A. after all
- B. all in all
- C. above all
- D. all over

> [!note]- 解析
> above all（首先、最重要的是）；after all 毕竟；all in all 总而言之。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语辨析。

**40.** Generally speaking, a business letter should be written in formal style ____ in a personal one.（==A==）

- A. rather than
- B. other than
- C. less than
- D. more than

> [!note]- 解析
> rather than（而不是）；other than 除了。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语辨析。


## Part II Cloze（完形填空，20分，20×1）

**Directions:** There are 20 blanks in the following passage. For each of the blanks there are four choices marked A, B, C and D. You should choose the ONE that best fits into the passage and mark the corresponding letter on the Answer Sheet.

Americans suffer from an overdose of work. Regardless (41) who they are or what they do, Americans spend (42) time at work than at any time since World War Ⅱ.

In 1950, the US had fewer working hours (43) any industrialized country. Today, it exceeds every country (44) Japan, where industrial employees load 2,155 hours a year compared (45) 1,951 in the US and 1,603 in the (46) West Germany.

Between 1969 and 1989, employed Americans (47) an average of 138 hours to their yearly work schedules. The workweek (48) above 40 hours, but people are working more weeks each year. (49), paid time off holidays, vacations, sick leave shrank (50) 15 percent in the 1990s.

As corporations have (51) stiffer competition and slower growth in productivity, they have pressed employees to work longer. Cost-cutting lay-offs in the 1990s (52) the professional and managerial runs, (53) fewer people to get the job done. In lower-paid occupations, (54) wages have been reduced, workers have added hours (55) overtime or extra jobs to (56) their living standard. The government estimates that more than seven million people hold a second job.

For the first time, large (57) of people say they want to cut (58) on working hours, even if it means earning less money. (59) most employers are unwilling to let them do so. The government, which has stepped back from its traditional (60) as a regulator of work time, should take steps to make shorter hours possible.

**41.** Regardless (　) who they are or what they do...（==A==）

- A. of
- B. with
- C. for
- D. by

> [!note]- 解析
> regardless of（不管、无论）为固定搭配。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> regardless of 搭配。

**42.** Americans spend (　) time at work than...（==C==）

- A. less
- B. same
- C. more
- D. much

> [!note]- 解析
> than 提示比较级，overdose of work 表明花更多时间，more。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 比较级。

**43.** ...had fewer working hours (　) any industrialized country.（==C==）

- A. to
- B. like
- C. than
- D. among

> [!note]- 解析
> fewer…than（比……更少）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 比较级连词。

**44.** Today, it exceeds every country (　) Japan...（==B==）

- A. and
- B. but
- C. as
- D. or

> [!note]- 解析
> exceeds every country but Japan（除日本外超过所有国家），but 表“除……之外”。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> but 作介词。

**45.** ...compared (　) 1,951 in the US...（==B==）

- A. for
- B. with
- C. in
- D. at

> [!note]- 解析
> compared with（与……相比）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> compared with。

**46.** ...1,603 in the (　) West Germany.（==D==）

- A. past
- B. late
- C. earlier
- D. former

> [!note]- 解析
> the former West Germany（前西德）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词词义。

**47.** ...employed Americans (　) an average of 138 hours to their yearly work schedules.（==D==）

- A. added
- B. increased
- C. brought
- D. totaled

> [!note]- 解析
> totaled an average of 138 hours（总计平均达138小时）；add to 搭配需 add sth. to。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**48.** The workweek (　) above 40 hours...（==A==）

- A. stood
- B. stopped
- C. remained
- D. set

> [!note]- 解析
> stand above 40 hours（停留在40小时以上），stand 表“处于某水平”。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**49.** (　), paid time off... shrank...（==D==）

- A. However
- B. Nevertheless
- C. Therefore
- D. Moreover

> [!note]- 解析
> 递进：工作周长之外，带薪休假还在缩水，用 Moreover。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 逻辑连接词。

**50.** ...sick leave shrank (　) 15 percent in the 1990s.（==A==）

- A. by
- B. at
- C. with
- D. below

> [!note]- 解析
> shrink by + 百分比（缩减了……）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 介词 by 表幅度。

**51.** As corporations have (　) stiffer competition...（==B==）

- A. suffered
- B. experienced
- C. undertaken
- D. endured

> [!note]- 解析
> experienced stiffer competition（经历更激烈的竞争）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**52.** ...lay-offs in the 1990s (　) the professional and managerial runs...（==B==）

- A. shortened
- B. reduced
- C. lessened
- D. relieved

> [!note]- 解析
> lay-offs 减少了管理岗位，reduced（减少数量/规模）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**53.** ...(　) fewer people to get the job done.（==A==）

- A. leaving
- B. left
- C. leave
- D. to leave

> [!note]- 解析
> 现在分词 leaving 作结果状语，留下更少的人完成工作。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 现在分词作结果状语。

**54.** In lower-paid occupations, (　) wages have been reduced...（==B==）

- A. which
- B. where
- C. while
- D. though

> [!note]- 解析
> where 引导定语从句修饰 occupations（在这些低薪职业中）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 定语从句关系副词。

**55.** ...workers have added hours (　) overtime or extra jobs...（==D==）

- A. by
- B. for
- C. to
- D. in

> [!note]- 解析
> in overtime（在加班中）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 介词。

**56.** ...or extra jobs to (　) their living standard.（==A==）

- A. preserve
- B. conserve
- C. improve
- D. protect

> [!note]- 解析
> 工资下降后加班是为了维持（preserve）原有的生活水平，而非提高。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

> [!warning]+ ⚠ 答案存疑
> 好老师答案 key 给 A(preserve)；语义上 improve 也通顺，但与“wages reduced”呼应，官方取 preserve。

**57.** ...large (　) of people say they want to cut...（==A==）

- A. numbers
- B. amounts
- C. figures
- D. quantity

> [!note]- 解析
> large numbers of + 可数名词复数（许多）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词搭配。

**58.** ...they want to cut (　) on working hours...（==D==）

- A. off
- B. out
- C. in
- D. back

> [!note]- 解析
> cut back on（削减）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语动词。

**59.** (　) most employers are unwilling to let them do so.（==C==）

- A. For
- B. And
- C. But
- D. Furthermore

> [!note]- 解析
> 转折：工人想缩短工时，但雇主不愿。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 逻辑连接词。

**60.** ...stepped back from its traditional (　) as a regulator of work time.（==D==）

- A. rule
- B. function

## Part VI Writing（短文写作，20分）

**Directions:** For this part, you are supposed to write a composition on the topic **Is Frustration a Bad Thing?** You should write at least 120 words, and base your composition on the outline given in Chinese below.

3. 我的看法。

**101.** 作文题：Is Frustration a Bad Thing?（至少 120 词，按提纲写作）。

> [!warning]- 参考范文（非官方，仅供参考）
> （以下范文由编者依题目提纲撰写，仅供参考，非官方标准答案，亦非任何机构公布的范文。）
> **Is Frustration a Bad Thing?**
> In our daily life, frustration is something that nobody can avoid. Some people regard it as a bad thing. Whenever they meet with failure or difficulty, they feel disappointed and even lose heart, refusing to make any further attempt.
> However, more people hold a different view. They believe that frustration is not necessarily a bad thing. On the contrary, it can teach us a valuable lesson and help us grow up. A person who has experienced setbacks is often better prepared for future challenges, because pain and failure make him wiser and stronger.
> In my opinion, frustration itself is neither good nor bad. What really matters is our attitude toward it. If we face it bravely and learn from it, it will become a stepping stone on the way to success. Therefore, we should never fear frustration, but treat it as a chance to improve ourselves.

> [!note]- 解析
> 提纲式议论文：正方（认为是坏事）→ 反方（更多人不认为是坏事，挫折促人成长）→ 个人看法（态度决定一切，化挫折为阶梯）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 提纲式对比议论文，120 词以上，注意 On the contrary / In my opinion 等连接词。

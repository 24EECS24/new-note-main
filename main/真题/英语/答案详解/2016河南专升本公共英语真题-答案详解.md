## Part I Vocabulary and Structure（词汇与结构，40分，40×1）

**Directions:** There are 40 incomplete sentences in this part. For each sentence there are four choices marked A, B, C and D. Choose the ONE that best completes the sentence, and then mark the corresponding letter on the Answer Sheet.

**1.** Jane hadn't been to London before. ____ had her husband.（==A==）

- A. Neither
- B. Either
- C. So
- D. Or

> [!note]- 解析
> 前句为否定句，后句表“……也不”，用 Neither/Nor + 助动词 + 主语倒装；So 用于肯定句表“也”。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 否定倒装（Neither/Nor）。

**2.** No sooner had he arrived in Rome ____ he heard of the good news.（==B==）

- A. when
- B. than
- C. then
- D. until

> [!note]- 解析
> 固定句型 No sooner…than…（一……就……），No sooner 置于句首主句部分倒装。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> No sooner…than 固定句型。

**3.** I'm for the suggestion that a special board ____ to examine the problem.（==A==）

- A. be set up
- B. will be set up
- C. must be set up
- D. has to be set up

> [!note]- 解析
> suggestion 后的同位语从句用 (should) + 动词原形虚拟；board 与 set up 为被动，故 be set up。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 同位语从句虚拟语气（被动）。

**4.** The disabled children need many things, but ____, they need love.（==A==）

- A. first of all
- B. not at all
- C. after all
- D. all in all

> [!note]- 解析
> first of all（首先，最重要的是）；not at all（一点也不）；after all（毕竟）；all in all（总而言之）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语辨析。

**5.** The coal industry in the north of China is now barely half its ____ size.（==B==）

- A. formal
- B. former
- C. latter
- D. later

> [!note]- 解析
> former（以前的，昔日的）；formal（正式的）；latter（后者）；later（稍后）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形近词辨析（former/latter）。

**6.** The football player is hoping to ____ to another team soon.（==A==）

- A. transfer
- B. transport
- C. transplant
- D. transact

> [!note]- 解析
> transfer（转会、转移）；transport（运输）；transplant（移植）；transact（交易）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词形近词辨析。

**7.** ____ the garden, the old man went to have a rest.（==C==）

- A. Having been watered
- B. Watering
- C. Having watered
- D. Being watered

> [!note]- 解析
> water 先于 went 发生，且 the old man 与 water 为主动，用现在分词完成式 Having watered 作状语。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 非谓语动词（现在分词完成式作状语）。

**8.** Her ____ to the job left her with very little free time.（==A==）

- A. devotion
- B. faith
- C. trust
- D. interest

> [!note]- 解析
> devotion to（对……的奉献/投入）；faith/trust 后接 in；interest 后接 in。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词搭配（devotion to）。

**9.** The children have been ____ since their father left.（==C==）

- A. out of style
- B. under control
- C. out of control
- D. in style

> [!note]- 解析
> out of control（失控）；under control（在控制中）；out of style（过时）；in style（时髦）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 介词短语辨析。

**10.** It is reported ____ great changes have been made with the application of the new policy.（==B==）

- A. which
- B. that
- C. while
- D. what

> [!note]- 解析
> It is reported that…（据报道……），it 为形式主语，that 引导主语从句且不充当成分。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 主语从句（It 作形式主语）。

**11.** The manager promised to keep me ____ of how the project was going on.（==B==）

- A. be informed
- B. informed
- C. inform
- D. informing

> [!note]- 解析
> keep sb. informed of sth.（让某人随时了解某事），informed 为过去分词作宾补。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> keep + 宾语 + 宾补。

**12.** He announced that the company had been transferred to ____ city.（==A==）

- A. another
- B. others
- C. the other
- D. one another

> [!note]- 解析
> another（另一，后接单数可数名词）；others 后不接名词；the other（两者中的另一个）；one another（互相）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 不定代词/限定词。

**13.** A great cheer went up from the crowd as they caught ____ of the hero.（==C==）

- A. vision
- B. view
- C. sight
- D. glance

> [!note]- 解析
> catch sight of（看见、瞥见）；glance 须搭配 catch a glance of。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 固定搭配 catch sight of。

**14.** Physics ____ my favorite subject when I studied in the university.（==B==）

- A. were
- B. was
- C. is
- D. are

> [!note]- 解析
> 学科名词 Physics 作主语谓语用单数；时间状语 when I studied 为过去，故 was。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 主谓一致（学科名词）+ 时态。

**15.** ____ we can succeed or not depends on how well we cooperate with others.（==D==）

- A. Then
- B. Thus
- C. What
- D. Whether

> [!note]- 解析
> Whether…or not（是否）引导主语从句；if 不能引导位于句首的主语从句。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 主语从句连接词 whether。

**16.** I will lend you the book ____ you return it to me in time.（==A==）

- A. on condition that
- B. in case
- C. in order that
- D. so that

> [!note]- 解析
> on condition that（条件是……）；in case（以防）；in order that/so that（为了）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 条件状语从句连词。

**17.** Mr. Brown is supposed to ____ for Italy last month.（==B==）

- A. be leaving
- B. have left
- C. leave
- D. have been left

> [!note]- 解析
> be supposed to have done（本应已经做了……），与 last month 呼应；left for 为主动。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> be supposed to have done。

**18.** —Did you enjoy the TV program last night? —No, ____ not.（==A==）

- A. particularly
- B. obviously
- C. surprisingly
- D. normally

> [!note]- 解析
> not particularly（并不特别喜欢），为口语常用表达。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 副词辨析。

**19.** We are disappointed yesterday because it wasn't such a good dinner ____ she had promised us.（==B==）

- A. like
- B. as
- C. which
- D. what

> [!note]- 解析
> such…as…（像……那样的），as 引导定语从句并在从句中作宾语。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> such…as 定语从句。

**20.** She walked along the path, ____ her daughter close behind.（==A==）

- A. following
- B. follow
- C. to follow
- D. was followed

> [!note]- 解析
> she 与 follow 为主动，用现在分词 following 作伴随状语。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 现在分词作伴随状语。

**21.** She insisted that the seats in the theater ____ in advance to insure a better view.（==B==）

- A. booked
- B. be booked
- C. are booked
- D. were to book

> [!note]- 解析
> insist 后宾语从句用 (should) + 动词原形；seats 与 book 为被动，故 be booked。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 宾语从句虚拟语气（被动）。

**22.** We are sometimes ____ of selfishness in our lives, though we are mostly generous in personality.（==C==）

- A. miserable
- B. greedy
- C. guilty
- D. sorry

> [!note]- 解析
> be guilty of（对……感到内疚）；miserable 悲惨的；greedy 贪婪的；sorry 后接 about/for。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词搭配 be guilty of。

**23.** The teacher doesn't allow his students ____ on the exam.（==D==）

- A. cheated
- B. cheating
- C. cheat
- D. to cheat

> [!note]- 解析
> allow sb. to do sth.（允许某人做某事）；allow doing sth.（允许做某事，不接人）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> allow 用法。

**24.** There is a lot of evidence ____ too much stress is partly responsible for the disease.（==D==）

- A. what
- B. which
- C. as
- D. that

> [!note]- 解析
> that 引导同位语从句，解释 evidence 的内容，从句不缺成分。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 同位语从句。

**25.** "____ everyone's here," she said, "let's begin to discuss the solution to the problems we have found."（==B==）

- A. As far as
- B. Now that
- C. So far
- D. By far

> [!note]- 解析
> Now that（既然）引导原因状语从句；as far as（就……而言）；so far（到目前为止）；by far（……得多）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 原因状语从句连词。

**26.** Most computer users are ____ threat from computer viruses.（==D==）

- A. within
- B. upon
- C. towards
- D. under

> [!note]- 解析
> under threat（处于威胁之下）为固定搭配。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 介词搭配 under threat。

**27.** No matter how ____, it's impossible that he has never lost money.（==D==）

- A. a businessman smart is
- B. a businessman is smart
- C. smart is a businessman
- D. smart a businessman is

> [!note]- 解析
> how + 形容词 + a/an + 单数名词：how smart a businessman is。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> how 引导让步状语从句的语序。

**28.** David told the truth ____ to annoy her, and she was angry for being betrayed by her good friend.（==A==）

- A. on purpose
- B. in need
- C. in detail
- D. at all

> [!note]- 解析
> on purpose（故意地）；in need（需要）；in detail（详细地）；at all（根本）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语副词。

**29.** The classroom is quite clean ____ some waste paper on the floor.（==A==）

- A. except for
- B. besides
- C. except
- D. without

> [!note]- 解析
> except for（除去整体中的细节/美中不足）；besides（除……之外还）；except 后接同类排除。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> except for 用法。

**30.** The bad weather ____ the building program by several weeks.（==C==）

- A. went on
- B. put on
- C. set back
- D. built up

> [!note]- 解析
> set back（延误、耽搁）；go on（继续）；put on（穿上/上演）；build up（建立）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语动词。

**31.** They naturally hope that their choice of the play will be ____ with the school and parents.（==D==）

- A. pleasing
- B. welcome
- C. kind
- D. popular

> [!note]- 解析
> be popular with（受……欢迎）为固定搭配。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> be popular with 搭配。

**32.** These two pictures are so ____ that it's very difficult to tell them apart.（==D==）

- A. exact
- B. familiar
- C. likely
- D. similar

> [!note]- 解析
> similar（相似的），与 tell them apart 呼应。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词词义。

**33.** These tickets for the performance are ____ until the end of the month.（==B==）

- A. acceptable
- B. available
- C. advisable
- D. applicable

> [!note]- 解析
> available（可获得的、可用的）；acceptable（可接受的）；advisable（可取的）；applicable（适用的）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词词义辨析。

**34.** Please ____ the water tap when you have finished your washing.（==B==）

- A. turn on
- B. turn off
- C. turn out
- D. turn over

> [!note]- 解析
> turn off（关掉）；turn on（打开）；turn out（结果是）；turn over（翻转）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语动词。

**35.** Don't associate with bad boys ____ your whole life will be ruined.（==A==）

- A. or
- B. but
- C. and
- D. so that

> [!note]- 解析
> 祈使句 + or（否则）表警告；and 表顺承。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 祈使句 + or。

**36.** I shall have a companion in the house after all these ____ years.（==B==）

- A. alone
- B. lonely
- C. single
- D. simple

> [!note]- 解析
> lonely（孤独的，作定语）；alone 只能作表语，不作前置定语。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> lonely/alone 辨析。

**37.** You ____ if you had had higher score in the examination.（==B==）

- A. must have got scholarship
- B. would have got scholarship
- C. should get scholarship
- D. had got scholarship

> [!note]- 解析
> 与过去事实相反的虚拟，主句 would have done，从句 had done。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 虚拟语气（与过去事实相反）。

**38.** The bank has established ____ in many big cities in the world.（==D==）

- A. parts
- B. twigs
- C. benches
- D. branches

> [!note]- 解析
> branches（分支机构、分行）；parts 部分；twigs 细枝；benches 长凳。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义（branch 熟词生义）。

**39.** While studying, he financially depended ____ his wife.（==A==）

- A. on
- B. of
- C. to
- D. from

> [!note]- 解析
> depend on（依靠、依赖）为固定搭配。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词搭配 depend on。

**40.** Only in this way ____ out of the plan.（==B==）

- A. you can talk him
- B. can you talk him
- C. him you can talk
- D. him can you talk

> [!note]- 解析
> "Only + 状语"置于句首，主句部分倒装，助动词 can 提前；talk sb. out of sth.（劝某人不做某事）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> Only 倒装句。

**41.** (　) an event takes place, newspapers are on the streets...（==A==）

- A. Just when
- B. While
- C. Soon after
- D. Before

> [!note]- 解析

**42.** ...newspapers are on the streets (　) the details.（==B==）

- A. to give
- B. giving
- C. given
- D. being given

> [!note]- 解析
> 现在分词 giving 作伴随状语，报纸沿街报道细节。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 现在分词作伴随状语。

**43.** ...reports are on the spot to (　) the news.（==A==）

- A. gather
- B. spread
- C. carry
- D. bring

> [!note]- 解析
> gather the news（采集、收集新闻）；记者在现场采集新闻。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**44.** Newspapers have one basic (　), to get the news...（==D==）

- A. reason
- B. cause
- C. problem
- D. purpose

> [!note]- 解析
> basic purpose（基本目的），冒号后为目的解释。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义。

**45.** ...from those who make it to those who want to (　) it.（==C==）

- A. make
- B. publish
- C. know
- D. write

> [!note]- 解析
> 从制造新闻的人到想知道新闻的人，know 与 make 相对。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**46.** Radio, telegraph, television, and (　) inventions...（==B==）

- A. another
- B. other
- C. one another
- D. the other

> [!note]- 解析
> other inventions（其他发明）；another 后接单数；the other 特指两者之一。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 限定词。

**47.** (　), this competition merely spurred the newspapers on.（==A==）

- A. However
- B. And
- C. Therefore
- D. So

> [!note]- 解析
> 转折：其他媒体的竞争反而刺激了报纸，用 However。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 逻辑连接词。

**48.** ...to improve the (　) and thus the efficiency...（==D==）

- A. value
- B. ratio
- C. rate
- D. speed

> [!note]- 解析
> improve the speed（提高速度），与 efficiency 并列递进。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义。

**49.** Today more newspapers are (　) and read than ever before.（==C==）

- A. spread
- B. passed
- C. printed
- D. completed

> [!note]- 解析
> 报纸是“印刷并阅读”，printed 与 read 并列。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义/被动。

**50.** Besides keeping readers (　) of the latest news...（==D==）

- A. inform
- B. be informed
- C. to be informed
- D. informed

> [!note]- 解析
> keep sb. informed of（随时告知某人），过去分词作宾补。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> keep + 宾补。

**51.** ...today's newspapers (　) and influence readers about politics...（==C==）

- A. entertain
- B. encourage
- C. educate
- D. edit

> [!note]- 解析
> 报纸教育并影响读者，educate 与 influence 并列。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**52.** Newspapers influence readers' economic choices (　) advertising.（==B==）

- A. on
- B. through
- C. with
- D. of

> [!note]- 解析
> through advertising（通过广告），表方式。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 介词。

**53.** Most newspapers depend on advertising for their very (　).（==B==）

- A. forms
- B. existence
- C. contents
- D. purpose

> [!note]- 解析
> for their very existence（赖以生存），advertising 是生存之本。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义。

**54.** Newspapers are sold at a price that (　) even a small fraction of the cost...（==C==）

- A. tries to cover
- B. manages to cover
- C. fails to cover
- D. succeeds in

> [!note]- 解析
> 售价连一小部分成本都不能覆盖，fails to cover。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语逻辑。

**55.** The main (　) of income for most newspapers is commercial advertising.（==A==）

- A. source
- B. origin
- C. course
- D. finance

> [!note]- 解析
> source of income（收入来源）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词搭配。

**56.** The (　) in selling advertising depends on a newspaper's value to advertisers.（==D==）

- A. way
- B. means
- C. chance
- D. success

> [!note]- 解析
> the success in doing（做某事的成功），广告销售的成功取决于报纸对广告商的价值。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义。

**57.** This (　) in terms of circulation.（==C==）

## Part VI Writing（短文写作，20分）

**Directions:** In this part, you are required to write a composition with at least 120 words on the topic **The Attitude toward Crisis**. Please write it on the Answer Sheet.

**101.** 作文题：The Attitude toward Crisis（至少 120 词）。

> [!warning]- 参考范文（非官方，仅供参考）
> （以下范文由编者依题目要求撰写，仅供参考，非官方标准答案，亦非任何机构公布的范文。）
> **The Attitude toward Crisis**
> Crisis is unavoidable in everyone's life. It may come in the form of a sudden failure, an unexpected illness or an economic loss. What really matters is not the crisis itself, but the attitude we take toward it.
> When facing a crisis, some people feel frightened and lose heart. They tend to avoid the problem and let it grow worse. Others, however, regard crisis as a challenge. They stay calm, analyze the situation, and try their best to find a way out. As a saying goes, crisis often means danger mixed with opportunity. A positive attitude can turn a threat into a chance to grow stronger.
> In my opinion, we should never give up in the face of crisis. Instead, we should keep calm, learn from our mistakes, and take action step by step. Only by holding a positive attitude can we overcome difficulties and become more experienced.

> [!note]- 解析
> 观点类作文：引入（危机不可避免）→ 两种态度对比（消极逃避 vs 积极应对）→ 个人看法（保持冷静、化危为机），结尾用 Only by 倒装。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 观点类议论文（对比+个人观点），120 词以上，注意连接词与结尾倒装。

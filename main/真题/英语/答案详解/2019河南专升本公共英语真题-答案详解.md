## Part I Vocabulary and Structure（词汇与结构，40分，40×1）

**Directions:** There are 40 incomplete sentences in this part. For each sentence there are four choices marked A, B, C and D. Choose the ONE that best completes the sentence, and then mark the corresponding letter on the Answer Sheet.

**1.** Let's walk to the shops instead of taking the car, ___?（==A==）

- A. shall we
- B. don't we
- C. will we
- D. won't we

> [!note]- 解析
> Let's 开头的祈使句，反意疑问句用 shall we，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 反意疑问句（Let's...shall we）。

**2.** The travel agency is arranging for us ___ at a really nice hotel.（==C==）

- A. stay
- B. of staying
- C. to stay
- D. staying

> [!note]- 解析
> arrange for sb. to do sth. 安排某人做某事，用不定式，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> arrange for sb. to do。

**3.** I wouldn't want ___ of my parents to know I have a boyfriend.（==A==）

- A. either
- B. any
- C. neither
- D. none

> [!note]- 解析
> 父母两者中"任何一个"都不想让其知道，否定句用 either；neither/none 本身含否定与 wouldn't 重复，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 不定代词 either。

**4.** I am beginning to think that ___ people say about him is true.（==C==）

- A. which
- B. this
- C. what
- D. /

> [!note]- 解析
> think 后宾语从句中缺 say 的宾语，"人们关于他所说的话"，用 what，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 宾语从句连接词 what。

**5.** I ___ the whole of War and Peace by the time I was seven years old.（==B==）

- A. was reading
- B. had read
- C. had been reading
- D. had been read

> [!note]- 解析
> by the time I was seven（到七岁时为止）与过去完成时连用，且为主动，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 过去完成时（by the time + 过去）。

**6.** Paul is a real introvert in contrast ___ his brother Andrew.（==C==）

- A. with
- B. by
- C. to
- D. against

> [!note]- 解析
> in contrast to 与……形成对比，固定搭配，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 固定搭配 in contrast to。

**7.** If I ___ the lottery, I'd give some of the money to each member of my family.（==D==）

- A. win
- B. have won
- C. will win
- D. won

> [!note]- 解析
> 主句 I'd give 表与现在事实相反的虚拟，从句用过去式 won，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 虚拟语气（现在）。

**8.** Medicine should not be kept ___ it is approachable to children.（==B==）

- A. when
- B. where
- C. which
- D. that

> [!note]- 解析
> keep 后接地点状语从句，"放在儿童够得着的地方"，用 where，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 地点状语从句。

**9.** Most people are ___ in a dentist's office than in a doctor's office.（==D==）

- A. obviously nervous
- B. more obviously nervous
- C. more nervous obviously
- D. obviously more nervous

> [!note]- 解析
> than 用比较级 more nervous；副词 obviously 修饰比较级应置于其前，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 比较级 + 副词位置。

**10.** If we ___ enough rain last year, we could have gained a good harvest.（==D==）

- A. have
- B. had
- C. have had
- D. had had

> [!note]- 解析
> 主句 could have gained 表与过去事实相反，从句用 had done，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 虚拟语气（过去）。

**11.** We ___ today and I got into trouble because I hadn't done it.（==B==）

- A. had checked our homework
- B. had our homework checked
- C. were checked our homework
- D. have checking our homework

> [!note]- 解析
> have sth. done 使某事被做，"我们今天找人检查作业"，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> have sth. done。

**12.** Bill asked me why ___ to go shopping with me.（==A==）

- A. I hadn't invited him
- B. hadn't invited him
- C. I hadn't him invited
- D. hadn't him invited

> [!note]- 解析
> 宾语从句用陈述语序，且 invite 后接宾语 him，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 宾语从句语序。

**13.** After finishing her course, Rachel went on ___ a well-known lawyer.（==A==）

- A. to become
- B. becoming
- C. to becoming
- D. become

> [!note]- 解析
> go on to do 接着做另一件事（成为律师是另一目标）；go on doing 继续做同一件事，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> go on to do。

**14.** This is the book in ___ Foster described his experience of the war.（==D==）

- A. that
- B. what
- C. where
- D. which

> [!note]- 解析
> 介词 in 后接关系代词 which 引导定语从句（in which），故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 定语从句（介词+which）。

**15.** Never ___ a greater day in the history of mankind.（==B==）

- A. did there be
- B. was there
- C. there was
- D. there has been

> [!note]- 解析
> 否定词 Never 置于句首，句子部分倒装；there be 句型，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 否定词句首倒装。

**16.** Gymnastics ___ to activities which require skills such as speed and adaptability.（==B==）

- A. is referring
- B. refers
- C. referred
- D. has referred

> [!note]- 解析
> 泛指客观事实用一般现在时，gymnastics 作单数，refers to 指，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 一般现在时 + refer to。

**17.** Apple ___ to have put a lot of effort into developing wireless earbuds.（==D==）

- A. said
- B. it is said
- C. has said
- D. is said

> [!note]- 解析
> be said to do 据说……，固定结构，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> be said to do。

**18.** There are ___ Chinese restaurants in New York City as in Boston.（==A==）

- A. twice as many
- B. twice as much
- C. as twice many
- D. as twice much

> [!note]- 解析
> 倍数表达"twice as many + 可数名词复数 + as"，restaurants 可数用 many，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 倍数表达（twice as many...as）。

**19.** The old temple ___ roof was damaged in a storm is now under repair.（==D==）

- A. where
- B. which
- C. its
- D. whose

> [!note]- 解析
> 先行词 temple 与 roof 为所属关系，用关系代词 whose，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 定语从句（whose）。

**20.** The engineers are so busy that they don't have time for outdoor sports activities, ___ they have the interests.（==C==）

- A. wherever
- B. whenever
- C. even if
- D. as if

> [!note]- 解析
> "他们没时间参加户外运动，即使有兴趣"，even if 即使，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 让步状语从句。

**21.** Researchers have ___ to the conclusion that your personality is affected by your genes.（==A==）

- A. come
- B. got
- C. reached
- D. arrived

> [!note]- 解析
> come to the conclusion 得出结论，固定搭配，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词搭配（come to a conclusion）。

**22.** She kept practicing ___ she could win the National Speaking Competition.（==C==）

- A. to
- B. in order to
- C. so that
- D. because of

> [!note]- 解析
> 后接从句表目的"以便"，用 so that；in order to 后接动词原形，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 目的状语从句（so that）。

**23.** Carl wasn't very good at mountain climbing as he's afraid of ___.（==B==）

- A. highs
- B. heights
- C. highness
- D. heightening

> [!note]- 解析
> be afraid of heights 恐高，height 用复数 heights，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词固定搭配。

**24.** Harry and Sam both denied that the fight was their ___.（==D==）

- A. blame
- B. criticism
- C. cause
- D. fault

> [!note]- 解析
> fault 过失、过错；blame 责备（不能说 one's blame），故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义辨析。

**25.** The government should do more for ___ people.（==C==）

- A. usual
- B. everyday
- C. ordinary
- D. typical

> [!note]- 解析
> ordinary people 普通人；usual 通常的，everyday 日常的，typical 典型的，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词搭配。

**26.** Some drugs produce bad side ___.（==D==）

- A. consequences
- B. results
- C. products
- D. effects

> [!note]- 解析
> side effects 副作用，固定搭配，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> side effects。

**27.** It is wise to ___ your property against storm damage.（==A==）

- A. insure
- B. assure
- C. reassure
- D. ensure

> [!note]- 解析
> insure...against 为……投保以防……；assure 使确信，ensure 确保，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词搭配（insure against）。

**28.** Joe told me a joke today but I didn't ___ it at all.（==A==）

- A. get
- B. bring
- C. collect
- D. fetch

> [!note]- 解析
> get a joke 领会笑话的笑点，固定搭配，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义（get）。

**29.** I think she ___ her fortune in the shipping industry.（==C==）

- A. took
- B. won
- C. made
- D. saved

> [!note]- 解析
> make one's fortune 发财，固定搭配，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> make one's fortune。

**30.** I really don't ___ the point of taking the exam when you are not ready for it.（==B==）

- A. look
- B. see
- C. have
- D. mind

> [!note]- 解析
> see the point of 明白……的意义/道理，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词搭配（see the point）。

**31.** The debate will ___ place tonight.（==B==）

- A. be
- B. take
- C. have
- D. make

> [!note]- 解析
> take place 举行、发生，固定搭配，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> take place。

**32.** Ivan tells me he really ___ himself at your barbecue last week.（==B==）

- A. pleased
- B. enjoyed
- C. played
- D. interested

> [!note]- 解析
> enjoy oneself 玩得开心，固定搭配；last week 用过去时，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> enjoy oneself。

**33.** What's the ___ between New York and Chicago?（==C==）

- A. length
- B. space
- C. distance
- D. gap

> [!note]- 解析
> 两地之间的距离用 distance；length 长度，gap 差距，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义辨析。

**34.** They were accused of interfering in China's internal ___.（==B==）

- A. matter
- B. affairs
- C. thing
- D. concern

> [!note]- 解析
> internal affairs 内部事务，固定搭配，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> internal affairs。

**35.** Please don't forget to ___ your essays during Friday's lesson.（==B==）

- A. hand
- B. hand in
- C. Hand
- D. handle

> [!note]- 解析
> hand in 上交（作业），固定短语，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语（hand in）。

**36.** The teacher asked some questions to make ___ that the student understood the text.（==C==）

- A. good
- B. true
- C. sure
- D. believe

> [!note]- 解析
> make sure that 确保、弄清楚，固定搭配，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> make sure。

**37.** She is finding ___ with the way I do my hair.（==C==）

- A. flaw
- B. mistake
- C. fault
- D. error

> [!note]- 解析
> find fault with 挑剔、找碴儿，固定搭配，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> find fault with。

**38.** Joe stared at me for a moment and then reluctantly ___ his hand.（==C==）

- A. extended
- B. increased
- C. raised
- D. put

> [!note]- 解析
> raise one's hand 举手；extend 延伸，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词搭配（raise one's hand）。

**39.** The earth ___ the necessary amount of heat and light from the sun.（==B==）

- A. supplies
- B. receives
- C. provides
- D. requires

> [!note]- 解析
> 地球从太阳"接收"热量和光，receive 收到；supply/provide 提供（方向反），故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义辨析。

**40.** Slow down! I can't ___ with you!（==D==）

- A. get on
- B. put down
- C. drop out
- D. keep up

> [!note]- 解析
> keep up with 跟上；get on with 相处，drop out 退出，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语（keep up with）。


## Part II Cloze（完形填空，20分，20×1）

**Directions:** There are 20 blanks in the following passage. For each of the blank there are four choices marked A, B, C and D. You should choose the ONE that best fits into the passage and mark the corresponding letter on the Answer Sheet.

Today it (41) have a bad reputation as a cause of addiction and obesity (肥胖), but for a long time sugar was a luxury (奢侈品) and (42) the opinion of the medical profession it still should be.

During the nineteenth century, however, manufacturers discovered (43) of producing it in vast quantities and it has since become (44) of the staple (主要的) articles of diet, particularly (45) the lower social classes. It has the advantages of (46) comparatively cheap, easily digested, rich (47) energy and useful for flavoring. Its major (48) are that it lacks nourishing (营养的) quality except that of giving energy, and (49) of its attractive flavor it (50) to displace other much more valuable foods (51) the diet. Most serious of all is its adverse (52) on health, since excessive consumption can cause heart (53), obesity and dental decay. The latter is widespread among the inhabitants (居民) of western countries. From the very young to the very old, (54) anyone escapes. Yet if parents drastically reduced the (55) of confectionery (甜食) they allow (56) children to eat, the extent of dental decay would soon be controlled. And (57) they were to (58) down their own consumption of sugar, they would suffer much (59) from diseases resulting directly or indirectly from their (60) overweight.

**41.** Today it (　) have a bad reputation as a cause of addiction and obesity...（==B==）

- A. must
- B. may
- C. should
- D. could

> [!note]- 解析
> "如今它可能名声不好"，may have 表推测，选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 情态动词表推测。

**42.** ...but for a long time sugar was a luxury and (　) the opinion of the medical profession...（==D==）

- A. to
- B. on
- C. for
- D. in

> [!note]- 解析
> in the opinion of 据……之见，固定搭配，选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 介词搭配 in the opinion of。

**43.** ...manufacturers discovered (　) of producing it in vast quantities...（==C==）

- A. problems
- B. difficulties
- C. ways
- D. origins

> [!note]- 解析
> discover ways of doing sth. 发现大量生产的方法，选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义。

**44.** ...it has since become (　) of the staple articles of diet...（==B==）

- A. same
- B. one
- C. simple
- D. well-known

> [!note]- 解析
> one of the staple articles 主要食品之一，选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> one of + 复数名词。

**45.** ...particularly (　) the lower social classes.（==D==）

- A. between
- B. with
- C. to
- D. among

> [!note]- 解析
> 在多个阶层"之间"用 among（三者以上），between 用于两者，选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 介词 among/between。

**46.** It has the advantages of (　) comparatively cheap, easily digested...（==C==）

- A. be
- B. having to be
- C. to be
- D. have to be

> [!note]- 解析
> of 后接名词/动名词结构作 advantages 的同位列举，此处取 to be 表"（其优点是）价格便宜、易消化"，选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动名词/列举结构。

**47.** ...rich (　) energy and useful for flavoring.（==A==）

- A. in
- B. with
- C. to
- D. for

> [!note]- 解析
> be rich in 富含……，固定搭配，选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> be rich in。

**48.** Its major (　) are that it lacks nourishing quality...（==A==）

- A. disadvantages
- B. qualities
- C. characteristics
- D. uses

> [!note]- 解析
> 后文"缺乏营养"是缺点，disadvantages 劣势，选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义（语境）。

**49.** ...and (　) of its attractive flavor it tends to displace...（==B==）

- A. thanks
- B. because
- C. due
- D. opposite

> [!note]- 解析
> because of 由于，后接名词短语表原因，选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> because of。

**50.** ...it (　) to displace other much more valuable foods...（==A==）

- A. tends
- B. owns
- C. has
- D. is

> [!note]- 解析
> tend to do 倾向于、往往会，选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> tend to do。

**51.** ...it tends to displace other much more valuable foods (　) the diet.（==B==）

- A. out of
- B. from
- C. within
- D. of

> [!note]- 解析
> displace...from 把……从饮食中取代，选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词搭配 displace...from。

**52.** ...Most serious of all is its adverse (　) on health...（==D==）

- A. strength
- B. outcome
- C. result
- D. effect

> [!note]- 解析
> adverse effect on 对……的不良影响，选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词搭配 effect on。

**53.** ...excessive consumption can cause heart (　), obesity and dental decay.（==B==）

- A. broken
- B. disease
- C. break
- D. stop

> [!note]- 解析
> heart disease 心脏病，固定搭配，选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> heart disease。

**54.** From the very young to the very old, (　) anyone escapes.（==B==）

- A. no way
- B. hardly
- C. if
- D. most unlikely

> [!note]- 解析
> hardly anyone 几乎没有人（逃脱），选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 否定副词 hardly。

**55.** ...if parents drastically reduced the (　) of confectionery they allow...（==A==）

- A. amount
- B. variety
- C. kind
- D. quality

> [!note]- 解析
> reduced the amount of 减少……的量，选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词搭配 amount of。

**56.** ...they allow (　) children to eat...（==B==）

- A. /
- B. their
- C. the
- D. for

> [!note]- 解析
> allow their children 允许他们的孩子，用物主代词 their，选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 物主代词。

**57.** And (　) they were to (　) down their own consumption of sugar...（==C==）

- A. when
- B. as
- C. if
- D. however

> [!note]- 解析
> 虚拟条件句"如果他们减少自己的糖摄入"，were to 表假设，用 if，选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 虚拟条件句。

**58.** ...they were to (　) down their own consumption of sugar...（==C==）

- A. shut
- B. slow
- C. cut
- D. turn

> [!note]- 解析
> cut down 削减、减少（消费量），选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语（cut down）。

**59.** ...they would suffer much (　) from diseases...（==B==）

- A. least
- B. less
- C. having been
- D. more

> [!note]- 解析
> 减少糖摄入会少受疾病之苦，much less 更少，选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 比较级 less。

**60.** ...resulting directly or indirectly from their (　) overweight.（==D==）

- A. having
- B. gaining
- C. having been
- D. being

> [!note]- 解析
> their being overweight 为动名词的复合结构作 from 的宾语，选 D。

**Passage 1**

More and more people around the world are taking part in dangerous sports and activities. Of course, there have always been people who have looked for adventure—those who have climbed the highest mountains, searched into unknown parts of the world or sailed in small boats across the greatest oceans.

**Passage 2**

The year was 1932. Amelia Earhart was flying alone from North America to England in a small single-engined plane. At midnight, several hours after she had left Newfoundland, she ran into bad weather. To make things worse, her altimeter failed and she didn't know how high she was flying. At night, and in a storm, a pilot is in great difficulty without an altimeter. **Passage 3**

The Winter Olympics are also called the White Olympics. At this time, many colorful stamps are published to mark the great Games. The first stamps marking the opening came out on January 25, 1932 in the United States for the 3rd White Olympics. From then on, publishing stamps during the White Olympics became a rule.

During the 4th Winter Olympic Games a group of stamps were published in Germany in November 1936. The five rings of Olympics were sewn (缝制) on the front of the sportswear. It was the first time that the rings appeared on the stamps of the White Olympics.

**Passage 4**

Be ware of (提防) those who use the truth to deceive. When someone tells you something that is true, but leaves out important information that should be included, he can create a false impression.

For example, someone might say, "I just won a hundred dollars on the lottery. It was great. I took that dollar ticket back to the store and turned it in for one hundred dollars."

This guy's a winner, right? Maybe, maybe not. We then discover that he bought two hundred tickets, and only one was a winner. He's really a big loser!

He didn't say anything that was false, but he deliberately omitted important information. That's called a half-truth. Half-truths are not technically lies, but they are just as dishonest.

Untrustworthy candidates in political campaigns often use this tactic (策略). Let's say that during Governor Smith's last term, her state lost one million jobs and gained three million jobs. Then another term. One of her opponents ran an advertisement saying, "During Governor Smith's term, the state lost one million jobs!" That's true. However, an honest statement would have been, "During Governor Smith's term, the state had a net gain of two million jobs."

Advertisers will sometimes use half-truths. It's against the law to make false claims so they try to mislead you with the truth. An advertisement might boast, "Nine out of ten doctors recommend Yucky Pills to cure nose pimples." It fails to mention that they only asked ten doctors and nine of them work for the Yucky Corporation.

This kind of deception happens too often. It's a sad fact of life: Lies are lies, and sometimes the truth can lie as well.

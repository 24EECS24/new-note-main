## Part I Vocabulary and Structure（词汇与结构，40分，40×1）

**Directions:** There are 40 incomplete sentences in this part. For each sentence there are four choices marked A, B, C and D. Choose the ONE that best completes the sentence, and then mark the corresponding letter on the Answer Sheet.

**1.** There wasn't anyone in the room at that time, ___?（==C==）

- A. isn't there
- B. wasn't there
- C. was there
- D. is there

> [!note]- 解析
> 前半句 "There wasn't anyone" 为否定，反意疑问句遵循"前否后肯"，且与 there be 保持一致，用 was there，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 反意疑问句（前否后肯）。

**2.** It was so noisy that the speaker couldn't make himself ___.（==D==）

- A. hear
- B. hears
- C. hearing
- D. heard

> [!note]- 解析
> himself 与 hear 为逻辑上的动宾关系（使自己被别人听到），make oneself done 用过去分词，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> make oneself + 过去分词。

**3.** Walk another block and cross the traffic lights, and you will see the hotel ___ your left.（==B==）

- A. by
- B. on
- C. in
- D. for

> [!note]- 解析
> on one's left 为固定搭配"在某人左边"，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 介词固定搭配 on one's left。

**4.** Whatever is left over may be put into the refrigerator, ___ it will keep for two or three weeks.（==D==）

- A. when
- B. which
- C. that
- D. where

> [!note]- 解析
> 先行词 refrigerator 表地点，定语从句中缺地点状语，用关系副词 where（= in which），故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 非限制性定语从句（关系副词 where）。

**5.** So little ___ about the British history that the lecture was completely beyond me.（==C==）

- A. do I know
- B. I knew
- C. did I know
- D. I know

> [!note]- 解析
> "So + 副词"置于句首，主句部分倒装；由从句 was 知用一般过去时，助动词 did 提前，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> So...that 部分倒装。

**6.** Professor Smith had a little trouble ___ data for his research.（==C==）

- A. collect
- B. collected
- C. collecting
- D. to collect

> [!note]- 解析
> have trouble (in) doing sth. 为固定用法，动名词作介词宾语，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> have trouble (in) doing。

**7.** If only I ___ what you want now!（==B==）

- A. known
- B. knew
- C. had known
- D. have known

> [!note]- 解析
> If only 表与现在事实相反的愿望，用过去式（be 用 were），由 now 可知为现在虚拟，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> If only 虚拟语气（现在）。

**8.** David has been studying here for three years and by next August he ___.（==C==）

- A. will graduate
- B. will be graduating
- C. will have graduated
- D. will have been graduating

> [!note]- 解析
> by next August（到明年八月为止）与将来完成时连用，will have done，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 将来完成时（by + 将来时间）。

**9.** Busy ___ she is, Catherine still does a lot of reading and writing after work.（==A==）

- A. as
- B. however
- C. although
- D. since

> [!note]- 解析
> as 引导让步状语从句须倒装（表语 Busy 提前），although/however 不倒装，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> as 引导让步状语从句倒装。

**10.** John wishes he ___ in business instead of politics when he was at college.（==B==）

- A. majored
- B. had majored
- C. have majored
- D. major

> [!note]- 解析
> wishes 后接虚拟；由 when he was at college（过去）可知是对过去的假设，用 had done，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> wish 后宾语从句虚拟（过去）。

**11.** We still have two hours as the plane will not ___ until 10 o'clock.（==B==）

- A. take after
- B. take off
- C. take away
- D. take out

> [!note]- 解析
> 飞机"起飞"用 take off；take after 像，take away 拿走，take out 拿出，均不合，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语辨析。

**12.** I don't doubt ___ he will pass the examination.（==B==）

- A. why
- B. that
- C. when
- D. whether

> [!note]- 解析
> 否定句中 doubt 后接 that 引导的宾语从句（不译"是否"），故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> doubt 宾语从句连接词。

**13.** Although Peter was in a hurry, he stopped ___ an old lady with a heavy box.（==C==）

- A. helping
- B. helped
- C. to help
- D. help

> [!note]- 解析
> stop to do 停下手中的事去做另一件事；stop doing 停止正在做的事。此处"停下来去帮助"用不定式，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> stop to do / stop doing 辨析。

**14.** Last week, the teacher, as well as a number of her students, ___ to attend the meeting.（==A==）

- A. was asked
- B. were asked
- C. had asked
- D. had been asked

> [!note]- 解析
> as well as 连接并列主语，谓语与前面的 the teacher 保持一致（单数）；teacher 与 ask 为被动，由 last week 用一般过去时被动，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 主谓一致 + 被动语态。

**15.** Helen ___ a good job since she graduated from the university.（==B==）

- A. doesn't find
- B. hasn't found
- C. didn't find
- D. hadn't found

> [!note]- 解析
> since 引导的时间状语从句，主句用现在完成时，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 现在完成时（since）。

**16.** The reason why Beethoven went away to the country was ___ he was gradually going deaf.（==D==）

- A. since
- B. as
- C. for
- D. that

> [!note]- 解析
> The reason...was that... 为固定句型，表语从句用 that 引导（不用 why/because），故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 表语从句（The reason is that）。

**17.** We will go to visit the Great Wall next year ___ we have enough money.（==D==）

- A. unless
- B. lest
- C. until
- D. provided

> [!note]- 解析
> provided (that) 表"如果、假如"引导条件状语从句；unless 除非，lest 以免，until 直到，均不合，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 条件状语从句连接词。

**18.** Only when the war was over in 1945 ___ to his hometown.（==B==）

- A. he returned
- B. did he return
- C. he did return
- D. had he returned

> [!note]- 解析
> "Only + 状语从句"置于句首，主句部分倒装；由 was over（过去）用一般过去时 did 提前，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> Only 句首倒装。

**19.** ___ a wonderful trip he had in China!（==D==）

- A. Where
- B. How
- C. That
- D. What

> [!note]- 解析
> 感叹句结构"What + a/an + 形容词 + 名词 + 主语 + 谓语"，a wonderful trip 为名词短语，用 What，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 感叹句（What a + n.）。

**20.** Tom ___ his former English teacher when he was shopping the day before yesterday.（==D==）

- A. ran away
- B. ran up
- C. ran over
- D. ran into

> [!note]- 解析
> run into 偶然遇见；run away 逃走，run up 积欠，run over 溢出/碾过，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语（run into）。

**21.** When you have any difficulty, you could ___ your friends for help.（==A==）

- A. turn to
- B. turn out
- C. turn over
- D. turn on

> [!note]- 解析
> turn to sb. for help 向某人求助；turn out 结果是，turn over 翻转，turn on 打开，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语（turn to）。

**22.** My new job is good ___ salary, but it does not seem to have opportunities for promotion.（==C==）

- A. in charge of
- B. in case of
- C. in terms of
- D. in favor of

> [!note]- 解析
> in terms of 就……而言；in charge of 掌管，in case of 万一，in favor of 支持，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 介词短语辨析。

**23.** You will be able to pass the examination if you do not lose ___.（==B==）

- A. weight
- B. heart
- C. temper
- D. sight

> [!note]- 解析
> lose heart 灰心、丧失信心；lose weight 减肥，lose one's temper 发脾气，lose one's sight 失明，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语（lose heart）。

**24.** The policeman had to let him go, for there wasn't enough ___ to prove his guilt.（==C==）

- A. sign
- B. scene
- C. evidence
- D. symbol

> [!note]- 解析
> evidence 证据；sign 迹象，scene 场面，symbol 象征，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义辨析。

**25.** The employee's demand for a pay rise was ___ by his boss.（==A==）

- A. refused
- B. resulted
- C. reviewed
- D. requested

> [!note]- 解析
> refuse 拒绝；result 导致，review 复查，request 请求，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义辨析。

**26.** He is as ___ as a donkey, doing everything in his own way and refusing to listen to advice.（==B==）

- A. strict
- B. stubborn
- C. objective
- D. particular

> [!note]- 解析
> stubborn 固执的；strict 严格的，objective 客观的，particular 挑剔的，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词词义辨析。

**27.** After a long summer holiday, Tom finds it difficult to ___ himself to the busy life on campus.（==D==）

- A. adopt
- B. carry
- C. lift
- D. adjust

> [!note]- 解析
> adjust oneself to 使自己适应；adopt 收养/采用，carry 携带，lift 举起，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词搭配（adjust oneself to）。

**28.** Travelers had better get their reservations well ___ if they fly during the Christmas holidays.（==D==）

- A. above
- B. out
- C. with
- D. in advance

> [!note]- 解析
> in advance 提前、预先，固定搭配，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 固定搭配 in advance。

**29.** This dictionary is very useful and I often ___ the words I don't know in it.（==B==）

- A. look at
- B. look up
- C. look for
- D. look into

> [!note]- 解析
> look up（在词典中）查阅；look at 看，look for 寻找，look into 调查，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语（look up）。

**30.** Two ___ the students in our school will go to the summer camp.（==D==）

- A. hundreds of
- B. hundreds
- C. hundred
- D. hundred of

> [!note]- 解析
> hundred 前有具体数字用单数，后接特指名词（the students）时加 of，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 数词用法（two hundred of）。

**31.** I hear you are going hiking this weekend. Would you mind if I ___?（==D==）

- A. come on
- B. come up
- C. come out
- D. come along

> [!note]- 解析
> come along 跟随、一起来；come on 加油，come up 出现，come out 出版，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语（come along）。

**32.** Jenny came here to ___ that everything was all right.（==D==）

- A. think of
- B. try out
- C. think over
- D. make sure

> [!note]- 解析
> make sure 确保、查明；think of 想到，try out 试验，think over 仔细考虑，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语（make sure）。

**33.** Mrs. Smith is kind and always ___ her help to others.（==A==）

- A. offers
- B. makes
- C. shares
- D. receives

> [!note]- 解析
> offer sth. to sb. 主动向某人提供某物；make 制造，share 分享，receive 收到，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词搭配（offer to）。

**34.** It is too bright and sunny. Why not ___ your sunglasses?（==B==）

- A. convey
- B. wear
- C. break
- D. clean

> [!note]- 解析
> wear sunglasses 戴太阳镜；convey 传达，break 打破，clean 清洁，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义辨析（wear）。

**35.** In ___ most countries, a university degree can give you ___ flying start in your career.（==D==）

- A. the; a
- B. the; /
- C. /; /
- D. /; a

> [!note]- 解析
> most countries 表泛指不加冠词；a flying start 为固定短语"良好开端"，加 a，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 冠词用法。

**36.** If you find this question too difficult to ___, it is advisable to leave it as it is and move on to the next one.（==A==）

- A. work out
- B. work on
- C. work for
- D. work at

> [!note]- 解析
> work out 解决、算出；work on 致力于，work for 为……工作，work at 从事，故选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语（work out）。

**37.** ___ of the twins was arrested because I saw them both at a party last night.（==C==）

- A. None
- B. Both
- C. Neither
- D. All

> [!note]- 解析
> twins 为两者，由"我两个都见到了"可知两个都没被抓，用 Neither，故选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 不定代词（neither）。

**38.** If I take this medicine three times a day, it should ___ my cold.（==B==）

- A. heal
- B. cure
- C. treat
- D. recover

> [!note]- 解析
> cure 治愈（疾病）；heal 多指伤口愈合，treat 治疗（不强调结果），recover 康复（不及物），故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义辨析。

**39.** Being chased by a dog, Jack jumped ___ the fence.（==B==）

- A. up
- B. over
- C. on
- D. from

> [!note]- 解析
> jump over 跳过；jump up 跳起，jump on 批评，jump from 从……跳，故选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词搭配（jump over）。

**40.** Self-criticism is very necessary ___ it helps us to find and correct our mistakes.（==D==）

- A. by that
- B. at that
- C. on that
- D. in that

> [!note]- 解析
> in that 因为、由于，引导原因状语从句，故选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 原因状语从句（in that）。

**41.** But people now live no longer than they (　).（==C==）

- A. would
- B. be used to
- C. used to
- D. used

> [!note]- 解析
> "now live no longer than they used to (live)"，used to 表过去（常常），与 now 对比，选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> used to 用法。

**42.** Yet, all living things still show the (　) of aging...（==D==）

- A. function
- B. courage
- C. affect
- D. sign

> [!note]- 解析
> "show the sign(s) of aging" 表现出衰老的迹象，sign 迹象，选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义。

**43.** ...which will eventually result (　) death.（==D==）

- A. lead in
- B. give in
- C. run into
- D. result in

> [!note]- 解析
> result in 导致、造成（结果），此处表衰老最终导致死亡，选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语（result in）。

**44.** ...the cells of the body and the organs they (　)...（==D==）

- A. hands
- B. feet
- C. heart
- D. organs

> [!note]- 解析
> 与 cells 并列，指身体的"器官"，organs 为身体部位统称，选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义（语境）。

**45.** ...do not function as well as they (　) in childhood and adolescence.（==C==）

- A. do
- B. has done
- C. did
- D. had done

> [!note]- 解析
> 与过去时间 in childhood and adolescence 比较，用一般过去时 did，选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词时态（过去比较）。

**46.** The body provides less (　) against disease...（==B==）

- A. energy
- B. protection
- C. vigor
- D. power

> [!note]- 解析
> protection against disease 抵御疾病的防护，选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词搭配。

**47.** ...and is more inclined (　) have an accident.（==A==）

- A. to
- B. with
- C. for
- D. of

> [!note]- 解析
> be inclined to do 倾向于、很可能做，固定搭配，选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词搭配（be inclined to）。

**48.** A number of related causes may (　) aging.（==B==）

- A. attend to
- B. contribute to
- C. add to
- D. devote to

> [!note]- 解析
> contribute to 促成、是……的原因之一；句意"相关原因可能促成衰老"，选 B。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词短语（contribute to）。

**49.** ...but they are not (　) when they die.（==A==）

- A. replaced
- B. rebuilt
- C. recovered
- D. surrendered

> [!note]- 解析
> 由下文 replaced by new cells 原词复现，"死亡后不能被替换"，选 A。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词复现（replace）。

**50.** As a person ages, (　) of brain cells and muscle cells decreases.（==C==）

- A. a number
- B. the amount
- C. the number
- D. a great deal

> [!note]- 解析
> the number of + 可数名词复数，谓语用单数 decreases；amount/deal 接不可数，选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> the number of 主谓一致。

**51.** (　) body cells die and are replaced by new cells.（==D==）

- A. The others
- B. Others
- C. Another
- D. Other

> [!note]- 解析
> other 作形容词修饰 body cells，"其他的体细胞"，选 D。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 不定代词限定。

**52.** In an aging person the (　) cells may not be as viable...（==C==）

- A. old
- B. left
- C. new
- D. young

> [!note]- 解析
> 与"年轻人的细胞"对比，此处指（衰老者体内的）新细胞，选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词语境。

**53.** ...as capable (　) growth as 54 of a young person.（==C==）

- A. to
- B. for
- C. of
- D. in

> [!note]- 解析
> be capable of (doing) sth. 有……的能力，固定搭配，选 C。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词搭配（capable of）。

**54.** ...as capable of growth as (　) of a young person.（==D==）

- A. this
- B. that

## Part VI Writing（短文写作，20分）

**Directions:** For this part, you are supposed to write a composition on the topic: **How to Relieve Stress**. You should write at least 120 words, and base your composition on the outline given in Chinese below.

1. 现代人的压力越来越大；
2. 减压的方法有很多；
3. 你的看法。

**101.** 作文题：How to Relieve Stress（至少 120 词）。

> [!warning]- 参考范文（非官方，仅供参考）
> （以下范文由编者依题目材料与要求撰写，仅供参考，非官方标准答案，亦非任何机构公布的范文。）
> **How to Relieve Stress**
> Stress is an inevitable part of our life. Many kinds of big events, such as natural disasters, wars and deaths, can cause great stress. Moreover, on a day-to-day basis, some people may also be faced with stress, resulting from trivial things, such as exams, waiting in line, and having too many things to do within a limited time and so on.
> When it comes to reducing stress, people vary in their ways. Some people talk with someone, perhaps a friend or a family member, about their anxieties and worries. Others allow themselves a mental "getaway": a quiet country scene painted mentally can divert their attention from the tension of a stressful situation to a more relaxing frame of mind. There are also people who create a sense of peace by reading a good book.
> As far as I am concerned, when stress does occur, it is important to recognize it and deal with it rather than escape from it.

> [!note]- 解析
> 提纲式作文：引入（压力无处不在）→ 方法（倾诉/心理逃离/读书，用 Some...Others...There are also... 分层）→ 总结（As far as I am concerned）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 提纲式议论文，120 词以上，注意连接词。

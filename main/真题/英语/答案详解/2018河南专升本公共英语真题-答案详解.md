## Part I Vocabulary and Structure（词汇与结构，40分，40×1）

**Directions:** There are 40 incomplete sentences in this part. For each sentence there are four choices marked A, B, C and D. Choose the ONE that best completes the sentence, and then mark the corresponding letter on the Answer Sheet.

**1.** Lovely weather, ____?（==B==）

- A. is it
- B. isn't it
- C. right
- D. ok

> [!note]- 解析
> 陈述部分为肯定句，反意疑问部分用否定；Lovely weather 省略了 It is，故 isn't it。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 反意疑问句。

**2.** ____ at this, the situation doesn't seem so desperate.（==B==）

- A. Looking
- B. Looked
- C. Being looked
- D. To look

> [!note]- 解析
> look 与句子主语 the situation 构成被动关系，用过去分词 Looked 作状语。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 过去分词作状语。

**3.** Mathematics ____ the language of science.（==A==）

- A. is
- B. has been
- C. are
- D. have been

> [!note]- 解析
> 学科名词 Mathematics 作主语谓语用单数；陈述一般事实用一般现在时 is。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 主谓一致（学科名词）。

**4.** We have been there for ____ times.（==C==）

- A. hundred of
- B. hundreds
- C. hundreds of
- D. hundred

> [!note]- 解析
> hundreds of（数以百计的），固定用法。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 数词 hundred 的用法。

**5.** By the time you get to New York, I ____ for London.（==D==）

- A. would be leaving
- B. am leaving
- C. have already left
- D. shall have left

> [!note]- 解析
> By the time 后接将来时间，主句用将来完成时 shall have left。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 将来完成时。

**6.** ____ the whole situation, I wouldn't have said it.（==C==）

- A. If I should know
- B. If I knew
- C. If I had known
- D. If I were to know

> [!note]- 解析
> 主句 wouldn't have said 表与过去事实相反的虚拟，从句用 had known。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 虚拟语气（与过去事实相反）。

**7.** The master was very angry and had all the slaves ____ before him.（==C==）

- A. to be brought
- B. be brought
- C. brought
- D. being brought

> [!note]- 解析
> have sth. done（让某事被别人做），slaves 与 bring 为被动，用过去分词 brought 作宾补。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> have sth. done。

**8.** Never before that night ____ the extent of my own power.（==A==）

- A. had I felt
- B. I felt
- C. did I felt
- D. I had felt

> [!note]- 解析
> Never before 否定状语置于句首，部分倒装；before that night 用过去完成时 had I felt。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 否定状语倒装。

**9.** He is determined to get a seat for the concert ____ it means standing in a queue all night.（==B==）

- A. as if
- B. even if
- C. provided
- D. whatever

> [!note]- 解析
> even if（即使）；as if 好像；provided 假如；whatever 无论什么。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 让步状语从句连词。

**10.** I was very interested in ____ she told me.（==A==）

- A. all that
- B. all which
- C. all what
- D. that

> [!note]- 解析
> 先行词为 all（不定代词），关系代词只能用 that 引导定语从句。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 定语从句（all that）。

**11.** Only under special circumstances ____ to take make-up tests.（==A==）

- A. are freshmen permitted
- B. freshmen are permitted
- C. permitted are freshmen
- D. are permitted freshmen

> [!note]- 解析
> Only + 状语置于句首，主句部分倒装，are freshmen permitted。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> Only 倒装句。

**12.** What a lovely party! It's worth ____ all my life.（==A==）

- A. remembering
- B. to remember
- C. to be remembered
- D. being remembered

> [!note]- 解析
> be worth doing（值得做），主动形式表被动。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> be worth doing。

**13.** She ran back to the kitchen, eggs ____ carefully in her hands.（==B==）

- A. to be held
- B. held
- C. was held
- D. holding

> [!note]- 解析
> 独立主格：eggs 与 hold 为被动，用过去分词 held。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 独立主格结构。

**14.** It's necessary ____ the dictionary immediately.（==C==）

- A. that he will return
- B. that he returned
- C. that he return
- D. that he has returned

> [!note]- 解析
> It's necessary that… 从句用 (should) + 动词原形虚拟。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 主语从句虚拟语气。

**15.** The boy whom you lent the bike to ____ by a car.（==D==）

- A. hit
- B. be hit
- C. having been hit
- D. was hit

> [!note]- 解析
> 主语 the boy 与 hit 为被动，且为过去发生，用一般过去时被动 was hit。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 被动语态。

**16.** He will not be at the picnic, ____ to my disappointment.（==A==）

- A. much
- B. more
- C. too much
- D. much more

> [!note]- 解析
> to one's disappointment 前可用 much 修饰；much to one's disappointment。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 固定搭配。

**17.** In Britain, the best season of the year is probably ____ spring.（==D==）

- A. later
- B. last
- C. latter
- D. late

> [!note]- 解析
> late spring（暮春）；later 以后；last 最后的；latter 后者。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词词义。

**18.** This is ____ wonderful party!（==B==）

- A. so a
- B. such a
- C. such
- D. so

> [!note]- 解析
> such + a/an + adj. + 单数名词；so + adj. + a/an + 名词。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> such/so 区别。

**19.** I can hardly imagine ____ so much money on a coat like that.（==A==）

- A. spending
- B. spend
- C. to spend
- D. spent

> [!note]- 解析
> imagine doing sth.（想象做某事）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> imagine doing。

**20.** We have to take a taxi ____ we can arrive at the airport earlier.（==D==）

- A. as soon as
- B. in order to
- C. so as to
- D. so that

> [!note]- 解析
> so that 引导目的状语从句，后接从句；in order to/so as to 后接动词原形。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 目的状语从句。

**21.** There is a growing ____ of Australian wine among consumers.（==C==）

- A. relativity
- B. regularity
- C. popularity
- D. population

> [!note]- 解析
> popularity（受欢迎、流行）；relativity 相对论；regularity 规则性。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义。

**22.** Because of ____ of time, I didn't go shopping with my sister yesterday.（==A==）

- A. lack
- B. shortage
- C. zero
- D. poverty

> [!note]- 解析
> lack of time（缺乏时间）；shortage 指物资短缺；poverty 贫穷。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词搭配。

**23.** Jane, my closest friend, often ____ her secrets with me.（==B==）

- A. speaks
- B. shares
- C. reports
- D. finds

> [!note]- 解析
> share sth. with sb.（与某人分享）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> share with 搭配。

**24.** When he came for the interview, Sam felt ____ at first, but soon calmed down.（==A==）

- A. nervous
- B. exciting
- C. curious
- D. anxiously

> [!note]- 解析
> nervous（紧张的），与 calmed down 呼应；anxiously 为副词，不作表语。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词词义。

**25.** We would like you to know that we greatly ____ your timely help.（==A==）

- A. appreciate
- B. thank
- C. accept
- D. admire

> [!note]- 解析
> appreciate sth.（感激某事）；thank 后接人。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**26.** The manager told his secretary to ____ a contract.（==D==）

- A. draw in
- B. draw on
- C. draw out
- D. draw up

> [!note]- 解析
> draw up a contract（起草合同）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语动词。

**27.** Careless driving may ____ your life.（==A==）

- A. cost
- B. spend
- C. pay
- D. buy

> [!note]- 解析
> cost（使付出代价，主语为物）；spend/pay 主语为人。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**28.** We ____ have thirty full-time and fifteen part-time employees in our company.（==A==）

- A. currently
- B. mostly
- C. likely
- D. internationally

> [!note]- 解析
> currently（目前）；mostly 主要地；likely 可能地。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 副词词义。

**29.** The ____ of my new job were better than the old one.（==A==）

- A. conditions
- B. position
- C. boss
- D. pay

> [!note]- 解析
> conditions（工作条件/待遇）；由 were 提示复数。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义。

**30.** The rich man ____ his poor neighbours.（==B==）

- A. looked down to
- B. looked down upon
- C. saw down to
- D. saw down on

> [!note]- 解析
> look down upon（看不起）为固定短语。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语动词。

**31.** Hard work can often make ____ a lack of intelligence.（==B==）

- A. make for
- B. make up for
- C. make at
- D. make out at

> [!note]- 解析
> make up for（弥补）；make for 有助于。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 短语动词。

**32.** The car was badly smashed up, but the driver got away without serious ____.（==D==）

- A. damage
- B. wound
- C. pain
- D. injury

> [!note]- 解析
> injury（伤害、受伤）；damage 损害（物）；wound 伤口（刀枪伤）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 名词词义。

**33.** You must give us more time, ____ we shall not be able to make a good job of it.（==A==）

- A. otherwise
- B. whether
- C. therefore
- D. consequently

> [!note]- 解析
> otherwise（否则）；therefore/consequently 因此。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 连接副词。

**34.** You may speak ____; say what you like.（==C==）

- A. readily
- B. willingly
- C. freely
- D. independently

> [!note]- 解析
> speak freely（畅所欲言）；readily 乐意地；willingly 愿意地。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 副词词义。

**35.** The autumn air felt ____ she went to fetch a coat for her son.（==A==）

- A. chilly
- B. shivery
- C. chill
- D. cool

> [!note]- 解析
> chilly（寒冷的）；shivery 毛骨悚然的；chill 作名词/动词；cool 凉爽的。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词词义。

**36.** He is very ____ about his ugly appearance.（==B==）

- A. sensible
- B. sensitive
- C. senseless
- D. sentimental

> [!note]- 解析
> be sensitive about（对……敏感/在意）；sensible 明智的。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 形容词搭配。

**37.** Since she is angry, we ____ leave her alone.（==D==）

- A. had better to
- B. had rather
- C. might have
- D. might as well

> [!note]- 解析
> might as well（不妨）；had better 后接动词原形不带 to。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 情态动词短语。

**38.** Because the company was doing more business, it was necessary to ____ the factory.（==A==）

- A. extend
- B. increase
- C. rise
- D. grow

> [!note]- 解析
> extend（扩建、扩展）；increase 增加（数量）；rise 上升。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**39.** Breakfast is ____ in the dining room from 8 to 9:30 a.m.（==A==）

- A. served
- B. made
- C. cooked
- D. eaten

> [!note]- 解析
> serve breakfast（供应早餐）。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

**40.** The date of the meeting has been ____ from the 5th to 8th June.（==A==）

- A. postponed
- B. advanced
- C. put forward
- D. brought of

> [!note]- 解析
> postpone（推迟）；advance/put forward 提前。

> [!tip]- 考点/方法
> 动词词义。

## Part III Reading Comprehension（阅读理解，40分，20×2）

**Directions:** There are 4 passages in this part. Each passage is followed by some questions or incomplete sentences. For each of them there are 4 choices marked A, B, C and D. You should decide on the best choice and mark the corresponding letter on the Answer Sheet.

**Passage One**

Holiday Accommodation Parks, sometimes referred to as motor camps, are found in towns, resorts and rural areas, and are near a national park and a natural scenic spot. As well as offering power and cabin sites, accommodation can include On-site Caravans, Standard and Kitchen Cabins and Ensuite Units.

Holiday Parks have central facilities for showers, toilets, cooking and laundry and most have recreational facilities such as playgrounds, TV and games rooms. For overnight and camper-bums, and holiday makers, holiday parks provide safe protection for your tent; they can let visitors use rubbish stations, laundry and toilets. Many holiday parks provide self-contained units with en suite facilities and some are fully self-contained with TV, kitchen, bathroom and usually no telephones.

Backpacker hostels are generally independently owned where the emphasis is on getting travellers together. Most offer a mixture of shared rooms (dormitories) which may be single-sex on request. Double, twin and single rooms are also available.

**Passage Two**

**Chinese Hoi Sin Chicken with Asian Noodle Salad**
Serves 4–6, prep with 25 minutes, cook 1 hour

**Ingredients**
1.5kg Chicken drumsticks (12 drumsticks)
2 teaspoons onion powder
Salad
1/2 diced green onion, green onion, finely sliced
1/2 small Savoy cabbage (700g), finely shredded
2 green spring onions, finely sliced
1 medium red chili, deseeded, finely sliced
100g dried rice noodles, chopped
1/2 cup roasted almonds, chopped
**Dressing**
1/2 cup hoisin sauce (about 120ml)
1/3 cup soy sauce (about 80ml)
1/2 cup lemon juice (about 80ml)
2 teaspoons finely grated fresh ginger
3 cloves garlic, crushed

**Method**
(1) To make dressing, combine all ingredients in a jug. Mix well.
(2) Toss chicken with onion powder. Place in a single layer in an oiled, large roasting pan.
(3) Cook in a hot oven (200C), turning halfway and brushing with pan juices, for 40 minutes. Remove pan from oven. Pour one-third of dressing over chicken. Toss well to coat.
(4) Return pan to oven. Cook, turning halfway and brushing with pan juices, for a further 20 minutes or until chicken is cooked.
(5) To make salad, combine all ingredients in a bowl with remaining dressing. Toss well.
(6) Serve salad with chicken. Garnish with green spring onions.

**Passage Four**

**FEED THE BRAIN, BUT LESS SUGAR**

While no single factor has been identified as the cause of Alzheimer's, it is thought that lifestyle choices, especially exercise and sleep habits, can have a significant impact on the disease. A study by the Albert Einstein College of Medicine has suggested that lifestyle changes related to healthy aging look "more promising than drug studies."

For the last decade or so, researchers have been exploring the link between our modern diet and Alzheimer's disease, specifically the connection between sugar and the disease. A study published in the New England Journal of Medicine demonstrated that even a mild elevation of blood sugar is associated with an elevated risk of dementia.

Earlier research had shown that diabetics are more likely to develop Alzheimer's, but more recent research has shed further light on this: finding an intricate link between insulin resistance and the brain.

Our bodies produce insulin that is necessary for the survival of brain cells, but this process goes awry when a toxic protein called ADDL removes receptors from these cells and renders them insulin-resistant.

As the ADDL accumulates, our memory begins to deteriorate.

So what should we be feeding our bodies to give our brain the best chance? A diet low in sugar, rich in whole carbohydrates, nuts and healthy fat is essential. Opt for "real food," avoid processed options and try to keep added sugar levels to a minimum (ideally 15 and 25 grams a day). Add plenty of good fats like avocados, nuts and coconut and other nuts oil to the diet and try a high-quality probiotic to optimise the flora in the gut.

## Part V Error Correction（短文/单句改错，10分，10×1）

**Directions:** There are 10 sentences in this part. Each of the following sentences has four underlined parts marked A, B, C and D. You are required to identify the incorrect part, then write the corresponding letter and the correct answer on the Answer Sheet.

**91.** You **(A)** smash up my car, and **(B)** then expects me **(C) paying** for the repairs **(D)**.

> [!note]- 答案
> C 改为 to pay

> [!note]- 解析
> expect sb. to do sth.（期待某人做某事），expect 后接不定式，不接 doing。

**92.** The book will **(A)** show the readers **(B) how that** they **(C) have observed** can be used in other **(D) contexts**.

> [!note]- 答案
> B 改为 what

> [!note]- 解析
> show sb. 后接宾语从句，从句中 observed 缺宾语，应用 what；how that 不能引导。

**93.** On the grassland **(A) lays** the farmer and his **(B) family**, **(C) enjoying** the sunshine happily **(D)**.

> [!note]- 答案
> A 改为 lay

> [!note]- 解析
> 地点状语 On the grassland 置于句首，句子全部倒装；lie（躺）的过去式为 lay，lays 为第三人称单数，误用。

**94.** He will **(A)** surely finish the job on time **(B)**, **(C) in** that he is left to **(D)** do it in his own way.

> [!note]- 答案
> C 改为 provided

> [!note]- 解析
> 逻辑应为条件：只要让他按自己的方式做，他一定能按时完成；provided（只要）引导条件状语从句，in that（因为）不合逻辑。

**95.** **(A)** I can't boot my computer now. **(B)** Something must have **(C) gone** wrong **(D) in** its operation system.

> [!note]- 答案
> D 改为 with

> [!note]- 解析
> go wrong with（……出故障）为固定搭配。

**96.** Bob fails to **(A) attend** the **(B) evening** school. He **(C) may** be sick, because he never **(D) asks for** leave.

> [!note]- 答案
> C 改为 must

> [!note]- 解析
> because he never asks for leave 给出了肯定依据，对现在有把握的推测用 must（一定），may（可能）语气不足。

**97.** The doctor attempted to **(A) reason** the girl **(B) from** her anxiety; **(C) nevertheless**, he failed in the **(D) end**.

> [!note]- 答案
> B 改为 out of

> [!note]- 解析
> reason sb. out of sth.（劝说某人摆脱某事）为固定搭配。

**98.** **(A) While** taking down his **(B) instructions**, you should **(C) take care** not to **(D) leave away** any sentences.

> [!note]- 答案
> D 改为 leave out

> [!note]- 解析

**99.** **(A) While** we are appreciating the good quality of your **(B) black tea**, we **(C) regret** that your price appears to be **(D) highly**.

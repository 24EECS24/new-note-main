- [*] 例1.28:求极限 $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 1^-} \ln x \ln(1-x)$
?
`````col
````col-md
flexGrow=10
===

==分析:==
$$
\begin{align*}
&该极限为0\cdot\infty型,先利用等价无穷小替换简化复杂因式:\\[4pt]
&当x\to1时,令u=x-1\to0,得\ln x = \ln(1+(x-1)) \sim x-1\\[4pt]
&再做变量替换转化为\frac{\infty}{\infty}型计算即可\\[8pt]
\end{align*}
$$

==解:==
$$
\begin{align*}
&原式 = \lim_{x \to 1^-} (x-1)\ln(1-x)\\[8pt]
&令t=1-x,当x \to 1^-时t \to 0^+,此时x-1=-t,代入得:\\[4pt]
&原式 = \lim_{t \to 0^+} (-t)\ln t = -\lim_{t \to 0^+} \frac{\ln t}{\frac{1}{t}}\\[4pt]
&\xlongequal{\text{洛必达法则}} -\lim_{t \to 0^+} \frac{\frac{1}{t}}{-\frac{1}{t^2}} = \lim_{t \to 0^+} t = 0\\[8pt]
\end{align*}
$$
`````

另一解法：
$$
\begin{align*}
\text{原式}&=\lim_{x \to 1^-} \frac{\ln(1-x)}{\dfrac{1}{\ln x}}\\[6pt]
&=\lim_{x \to 1^-} \frac{-x}{\dfrac{1}{x-1}}\\[6pt]
&=\lim_{x \to 1^-} \frac{-1}{-\dfrac{1}{(x-1)^2}}\\[6pt]
&=0
\end{align*}

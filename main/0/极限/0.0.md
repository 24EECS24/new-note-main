- [*] **题目**：求极限$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^2 - 1}{\sin\left(\sqrt{x} - 1\right)}$ 
?
`````col
````col-md
flexGrow=10
===

$$
\begin{align*}
&\text{当} \ x \to 1 \ \text{时，} \sqrt{x}-1 \to 0, \text{由等价无穷小} \sin u \sim u \ (u \to 0)\\[4pt]
&\text{原式}= \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^2 - 1}{\sqrt{x} - 1}\\[4pt]
&\text{令} \ \sqrt{x} = t, \ \text{则} \ x = t^2, \ \text{当} \ x \to 1 \ \text{时} \ t \to 1, \ \text{且} \ x^2 = t^4\\[4pt]
&\therefore \text{原式}= \lim_{t \to 1} \frac{t^4 - 1}{t - 1}\\[4pt]
&\text{该极限为} \frac{0}{0} \ \text{型，应用洛必达法则对分子分母分别求导}\\[4pt]
&= \lim_{t \to 1} \frac{4t^3}{1}\\[4pt]
&= 4
\end{align*}
$$
**另一解法** 
$$
\begin{align*}
\text{原式} &= \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^2 - 1}{\sqrt{x} - 1} \\[4pt]
&\text{应用洛必达法则} \\[4pt]
&= \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{2x}{\frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}} \\[4pt]
&= \lim_{x \to 1} 4x\sqrt{x} \\[4pt]
&= 4
\end{align*}
$$
`````

- [*] 例1.31:求极限 $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty}\left[x^2\left(e^{\frac{1}{x}}-1\right)-x\right]$ 
?
`````col
````col-md
flexGrow=10
===

==分析:==
$$
\begin{align*}
&表达式无分母,先提取公因式构造分式,\\[4pt]
&再做倒代换t=\frac{1}{x}转化为\frac{0}{0}型,用泰勒展开计算即可\\[8pt]
\end{align*}
$$

==解:==
$$
\begin{align*}
&原式 = \lim_{x \to +\infty} x\left[x\left(e^{\frac{1}{x}}-1\right)-1\right] = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{x\left(e^{\frac{1}{x}}-1\right)-1}{\frac{1}{x}}\\[8pt]
&令t=\frac{1}{x},x\to+\infty时t\to0^+,代入得:\\[4pt]
&原式 = \lim_{t \to 0^+} \frac{e^t -1 -t}{t^2}\\[8pt]
&由e^t = 1 + t + \frac{t^2}{2} + o(t^2),得e^t -1 -t \sim \frac{t^2}{2}\\[4pt]
&故原式 = \lim_{t \to 0^+} \frac{\frac{1}{2}t^2 + o(t^2)}{t^2} = \frac{1}{2}\\[8pt]
\end{align*}
$$
**另一写法** 
当$x \to 0$时，指数函数的麦克劳林展开式为：
$$
e^x - 1 = x + \frac{1}{2}x^2 + o(x^2)
$$
$$
e^{\frac{1}{x}} - 1 = \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{2x^2} + o\left( \frac{1}{x^2} \right)
$$
计算极限：
$$
\begin{align*}
&\lim_{x \to +\infty} \left[ x^2\left(e^{\frac{1}{x}} - 1\right) - x \right]\\[4pt]
&= \lim_{x \to +\infty} \left[ x^2\left( \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{2x^2} + o\left(\frac{1}{x^2}\right) \right) - x \right]\\[4pt]
&= \lim_{x \to +\infty} \left( x + \frac{1}{2} + o(1) - x \right)\\[4pt]
&= \lim_{x \to +\infty} \left( \frac{1}{2} + o(1) \right)\\[4pt]
&= \frac{1}{2}
\end{align*}
$$
`````

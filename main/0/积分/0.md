**证明：** 
∵ 闭区间上的连续函数必有原函数
∴ 设$\displaystyle G(x) = \int f(t) \, dt = \int_{a}^{x} f(t) \, dt,\quad x\in[a,b]$

则$G(a) = 0$
$$G(b) = \int_{a}^{b} f(t) \, dt = G(b) - G(a)$$

∵ $F(x)$为$f(x)$的一个原函数
∴ $\displaystyle F(x) = \int f(t) \, dt$，即$F'(x) = f(x)$

∴ $F(x) - G(x) = C$
即$G(x) = F(x) + C$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\therefore \int_{a}^{b} f(t) \, dt &= G(b) - G(a) \\
&= \left[F(b) + C\right] - \left[F(a) + C\right] \\
&= F(b) - F(a)
\end{aligned}
$$

## 二、阅读理解四大题型回顾

进入细节题之前，顾老师先在黑板上带大家回顾阅读理解的四大题型，以及各自的标志词。

### 四大题型板书（黑板手写）

**【手写】阅读题型**

1. **细节题**：5W＋1H（when, what, where, why, who, how）
2. **推断题**：infer, conclude, imply
3. **猜词题**：① 下划线单词（解释说明 / 逻辑关系 / 构词法）；② 代词指代（就近）
4. **主旨题**

### （1）细节题

细节题是四大题型里占比最大的，大约占 **70%**。它的标志是特殊疑问词，也就是 **5W1H**：when、what、where、why、who 加上 how。

### （2）推断题

推断题的标志词是 **infer、conclude、imply**。

推断题只能进行**一步推理**：既不能推得太过分（脱离原文主观臆断），也不能直接照抄原文（照抄就不是推断了）。

### （3）猜词题

猜词题问下划线单词、短语或句子的含义，有三种解法：

1. **解释说明成分**：通过定语从句、同位语、特殊标点等解释性内容判断词义；
2. **逻辑关系**：借助 and、but、however、instead 等逻辑词推断；
3. **构词法**：通过词根词缀判断。

她以词缀 **-holic** 为例：-holic 表示“上瘾”，所以 **workaholic**（工作狂）、**shopaholic**（购物狂）。黑板右上角另写有 **【手写】praise-aholic kids**（渴望被表扬的孩子）。

**代词指代题**用**就近原则**：问文中 it、this 等指代谁时，往前找最近的名词，同时注意单复数是否一致。

### （4）主旨题

主旨题的标志是问 best main idea、suitable/best title 等，考查对全文中心的概括。

### 课程导学：划完关键词也不会定位

回顾完题型，课件给出本课要解决的核心问题。

**课程导学（课件）**

> 问题来了，划完关键词也不会定位……

配图为一只橘猫（满脸都写着高兴）。课件列了两道真题作为引子：

- What can we say about Baby Boomers?（2021 全国乙卷）
  - A. They like smartphone games.
  - B. They enjoy guessing callers' identity.
  - C. They keep using landline phones.
  - D. They are attached to their family.
- Which of the following is TRUE about the Lancaster Design job?（2021 年 3 月天津卷）
  - A. Applicants are expected to design furniture.
  - B. Work is arranged according to one's skill set.
  - C. It involves collecting furniture from homes.
  - D. It favors applicants who are homeless.

顾老师由此引出：很多同学认识单词、也会划关键词，但就是回原文定位不到、或者定位错。接下来用四种定位规则解决这个问题。

## 三、细节题四种定位规则

这是本讲第一板块的核心。顾老师按题干和选项的不同情况，讲了四种定位规则。

### 规则 1：题干意图不明确——借小介词 to / for 定位

**课件正文：**

> 1. 题干意图不明确
>
> 选项中并不总是有特殊疑问词，但通过表述中的 to, for 等介词，我们还是可以准确地判断题目的考查点。

**【手写】**在 to、for 上画圈。

口诀：**to 找目的，for 找对象**。

#### 例题 1-1：High School Research Program（for 找对象）

**文章（P3，考点 1-A）：**

> Instead of hitting the beach, fourteen high school students traded swimming suits for lab coats last summer and turned their attention to scientific experiments.
>
> The High School Research Program offers high school students guidance with researchers in Texas A&M's College of Agriculture and Life Sciences. Jennifer Funkhouser, academic adviser for the Department of Rangeland Ecology and Management, directs this four-week summer program designed to increase understanding of research and its career potential (潜能).

**题目：**

> The research program is chiefly designed for ______.

- A. high school advisers from Houston
- B. college students majoring in agriculture
- C. high school students from different places
- D. researchers at the College of Agriculture and Life Sciences

**答案：C**

**【手写】** chiefly 下注“主要的”；题目左侧写“划”“定”，句末写“人”。

##### 讲题路径

1. 题干没有特殊疑问词，只说 be designed for，关键要抓住小介词 **for**。
2. 口诀：**for 找对象（目标人群）**，所以这题问的是这个项目主要为谁设计、面向什么人。
3. 回原文定位：The High School Research Program **offers high school students guidance**——项目给高中生提供指导，服务对象就是高中生。
4. 逐一排除：
   - A 的 high school advisers（顾问）是提供服务、做指导的人，不是服务对象；
   - B 的 college students 是大学生，原文明确是 high school students；
   - D 的 researchers（研究员）也是提供指导的一方，不是项目面向的人。
5. 因此选 C：来自不同地方的高中生。

#### 例题 1-2：The Silence Machine（to 找目的）

**文章（P4，B）：**

> For those who hunger for some peace and quiet, sound can now create silence. Let's say you are at the airport, and the little boy on the seat next to you is humming (哼唱) a short commercial song. He hums it over and over again, and you are about to go crazy. Thanks to the Silence Machine, a British invention, you can get rid of the sound without upsetting the boy or his parents. One may wonder how the Silence Machine works. Well, it functions by analyzing the waves of the incoming sound and creating a second set of outgoing waves. The two sets of waves cancel each other out. Simply turn the machine on, point it at the target, and your peace and quiet comes back.

**题目：**

> The Silence Machine is a device specially designed to ______.

- A. silence the people around you
- B. remove the sound of commercials
- C. block the incoming sound waves
- D. stop unwanted sound from affecting you

**答案：D**

**【手写】** device 下注“设备”；在 to 上画圈。

##### 讲题路径

1. 题干是 be designed to，抓住介词 **to**。口诀：**to 找目的**，问的是这个设备设计出来的目的，而不是工作原理。
2. 回原文：Thanks to the Silence Machine ... you can **get rid of the sound** without upsetting the boy or his parents——目的是让你摆脱不想听的声音、不被它打扰，对应 D：stop unwanted sound from affecting you。
3. 逐一排除：
   - A“让周围的人安静”错，机器不是去封住别人的嘴，而是消除传到你这里的声音；
   - B“移除广告声”太窄，机场男孩哼的只是例子，机器能消除的是一切 unwanted sound，不限于广告；
   - C“阻挡传入的声波”是 **how（工作原理）**，原文 functions by analyzing the waves ... 讲的是它怎么工作，不是设计目的，属于答非所问。
4. 题目问的是目的，所以选 D。

**敲黑板：** to 找目的，for 找对象；看到 by、function 往往是在讲原理（how），不要把原理当目的。

### 规则 2：题干关键词模糊——顺序对应 + 简化选项

当题干关键词比较模糊、不好找到底问什么时（有些推断题也很像细节题），依据两条：

1. **顺序对应**：回到原文按顺序捋一遍，题目顺序与行文顺序基本对应，往下找、不回头；
2. **简化选项**：从选项里提炼几个关键词，带着关键词回原文找，速度更快。

#### 例题 2-1：Baggy 空气污染数据

**文章（P5，A）：**

> Baggy has become the first dog in the UK—and potentially the world—to join the fight against air pollution by recording pollutant levels near the ground.
>
> Baggy wears a pollution monitor on her collar so she can take data measurements close to the ground. Her monitor has shown that air pollution levels are higher closer to ground level, which has helped highlight concerns that babies and young kids may be at higher risk of developing lung problems.
>
> Conventional air pollution monitors are normally fixed on lampposts at about nine feet in the air. However, since Baggy stands at about the same height as a child in a pushchair (婴儿车), she frequently records pollution levels which are much higher than the data gathered by the Environment Agency.

**题目：**

> What can we learn from the Baggy data?

- A. High places are free of air pollution.
- B. Higher pushchairs are more risky for kids.
- C. Conventional monitors are more reliable.
- D. Air is more polluted closer to the ground.

**答案：D**

##### 讲题路径

1. 题干问 What can we learn，比较像推断、关键词也模糊，于是按顺序对应回原文捋，并简化选项抓关键词。
2. 原文 Her monitor has shown that **air pollution levels are higher closer to ground level**——越靠近地面污染浓度越高，直接对应 D。
3. 逐一排除：
   - A 说高处完全没有污染（be free of），过于绝对；高海拔只是污染浓度低，并非完全无污染；
   - B 说更高的婴儿车对孩子更危险，原文是说孩子所处高度（接近地面）污染重，并没有比较婴儿车高低；
   - C 说传统监测器更可靠，与原文相反，Baggy 测到的正是传统高处监测器漏掉的高污染数据。
4. 因此选 D。

**敲黑板：** 留意 show / find / argue / suggest / indicate / think 等词后面的宾语从句，那里极可能就是答案。

### 规则 3：选项信息不唯一（“吃了吐”）——盯转折、看后半段

**课件正文：** 前面明明说了一个事，后面突然来一个小转折把它否定。顾老师戏称这种题为“**吃了吐题型**”。

这种题如果只定位到前面的表述就会做错，必须留意出现转折的地方，以及后半段的总结性内容。

#### 例题 3-1：钢琴表演（演出前 / 演出中）

**文章（B）：**

> Slowly I walked to the mud-brown piano in the center of the room. It contained 88 demanding keys, which were waiting impatiently to be played. I swallowed the golf-ball-sized lump (隆起部分) in my throat and sat down. Slowly, I opened the music. Next, I rested my still shaking hands on the keys.
>
> As my fingers played across the keys, I was becoming more unsure of my preparation for this moment. But the memory of my years of training came flooding back. I knew that I had practiced this piece so many times that I could play it backwards if requested. Although at one point I accidentally played two keys instead of the intended one, I continued to move my fingers automatically. My eyes were fixed on the pages in front of me. There was no way that I was going to lose my concentration. To keep this to myself, I leaned forward and focused carefully on the music.

**题目：**

> The author ______.

- A. didn't make any mistake in the performance
- B. felt better at the beginning of the performance
- C. paid all attention to nothing but her performance
- D. lost her confidence gradually during the performance

**答案：C**

**课件提示：**

1. 简单分析 4 个选项可知，选项描述了作者**演出中**（演出前 / 演出中）的表现；
2. 由此可以定位到原文的**第 2 段**。

##### 讲题路径

1. 先看选项，四个选项都在讲作者的表现，要分清是演出前还是演出中；课件提示这是演出中，定位到第 2 段。
2. 第 2 段开头说 As my fingers played ... I was becoming more unsure（好像没把握），但紧接着 **But** 转折：多年训练的记忆涌回来，My eyes were fixed ... There was no way that I was going to lose my concentration ... focused carefully on the music。
3. 这正是“吃了吐”：前面说不确定，后面一个 But 否定，实际是全神贯注，对应 C：paid all attention to nothing but her performance。
4. 逐一排除：
   - A 说没犯任何错，原文明确说 accidentally played two keys（弹错了一个地方），不成立；
   - B 说一开始感觉更好，开头作者紧张到手抖、喉咙发紧，并不好；
   - D 说逐渐失去信心，But 之后是找回专注、没有失去 concentration，正好相反。
5. 因此选 C。

#### 例题 3-2：瑜伽 EXCEPT

**文章（考点 3-A）：**

> Samantha Tam said that practicing yoga improved her flexibility and treated her injuries. "Every time after practicing yoga, I always felt really relaxed and was in a good mood," she said.
>
> Yoga was probably introduced on a popular level in America in the 1960s and 1970s, when Western people began to show curiosity about other religions. Now most people pursue yoga to relieve stress, and fair numbers of people are simply curious.

**题目：**

> Today, people do yoga for many reasons EXCEPT ______.

- A. to get rid of stress
- B. to treat injuries
- C. to satisfy their curiosity
- D. to practice religion

**答案：D**

##### 讲题路径

1. 题干有 **EXCEPT**，要选“哪一个不是人们做瑜伽的原因”。
2. 回原文逐一找原因：
   - improved her flexibility and **treated her injuries**（对应 B）；
   - felt really relaxed、pursue yoga to **relieve stress**（对应 A，get rid of stress）；
   - show **curiosity** about other religions、simply curious（对应 C）。
3. 关于宗教，原文说西方人只是对其他宗教“好奇”、想去了解，并没有说做瑜伽是为了“练习/信奉宗教”，所以 D 的 to practice religion 不成立。
4. 这题也带“吃了吐”特点：宗教这个词出现了，但出现的语境是“好奇”而非“练习”，不能看到 religion 就选。
5. 因此选 D。

#### 例题 3-3：传统商业模式 EXCEPT（难题）

**文章（B）：**

> In a world with limited land, water and other natural resources, the harm from the traditional business model is on the rise. Actually, the past decade has seen more and more forests disappearing and the globe becoming increasingly warm. People now realize that this unhealthy situation must be changed, and that we must be able to develop in sustainable (可持续的) ways, or future generations will be faced with severe threats. That means growth with low carbon or development of sustainable products. In other words, we should keep the earth healthy while using its supply of natural resources.

**题目：**

> The traditional business model is harmful because of all the following EXCEPT that ______.

- A. it brings severe damage to future
- B. it is not sustainable
- C. it consumes natural resources
- D. it makes the world warmer

**答案：C**

**【手写】** C 项下注“不是不用自然资源，是合理利用”。

##### 讲题路径

1. 题干问传统商业模式有害，**EXCEPT** 选出“不是其危害原因”的一项。
2. 回原文核对：
   - future generations will be faced with **severe threats**（对应 A）；
   - 必须 develop in **sustainable** ways，说明当前模式不可持续（对应 B）；
   - the globe becoming increasingly **warm**（对应 D）。
3. 看 C：it consumes natural resources（消耗自然资源）。文章结尾说 we should keep the earth healthy **while using** its supply of natural resources——并不是完全不用自然资源，而是要**合理利用**。消耗资源本身不是错，错在“不合理”的消耗。
4. 所以 C 不是它有害的原因，选 C。

**敲黑板（EXCEPT 题）：** EXCEPT 题最容易因为思维惯性而选成“对的”。做题时要反复提醒自己：这题让我选的是“不对的 / 没提到的”，而不是选正确表述。

### 规则 4：选项需多次定位——四选项逐一回原文

当四个选项的信息相互独立、问“哪项正确 / 不正确”时，需要圈画四个选项的关键词，把 A、B、C、D 分别定位到原文，逐一对比信息正误。

**课件正文：**

> 4. 选项需多次定位
>
> 当四个选项的信息相互独立时，我们需要圈画四个选项中的关键词，分别定位到原文，对比信息正误。

**【手写】** not True，并列出 A、B、C、D。

#### 例题 4-1：Crystal Cruise 游轮广告

**文章（A）：**

> What do you plan to do this summer vacation? Do you want to travel over the sea, experience the ocean wave and enjoy the wonderful sea scenery? If that is what you want, come and sail on a Crystal Cruise ship (游轮). We have three ships: The Crystal Queen, The Crystal Princess, The Crystal Palace.
>
> Come and sail in luxury (奢侈) on cruises around the Caribbean Sea for 7 or 14 days.
>
> Our seven-day cruise costs 3,500. A typical one-week cruise:
>
> Day One — set off from Miami
> Day Two — free day in Nassau, in The Bahamas
> Day Three — near Haiti
> Day Four — visit Puerto Rico and Antigua
> Day Five — free day in Barbados
> Day Six — free day in Port of Spain, Trinidad
> Day Seven — travel to Caracas, Venezuela
> Day Eight — fly home.
>
> Our ships carry over 2,000 passengers and we have nearly 600 crew members in all. So come on board today for the holiday of a lifetime!

**题目：**

> Which of the following statement is TRUE according to the passage?

- A. Travelers need to pay $2,000 if want to sail in luxury on cruises for 14 days.
- B. Travelers can wander around Barbados on the third day.
- C. The ships can carry about 600 passengers.
- D. Travelers can wander around the Caribbean Sea.

**答案：D**

##### 讲题路径

1. 问 TRUE，四个选项信息独立，逐一回原文定位。
2. 逐项核对：
   - A 说 14 天豪华游轮付 2000 元；原文 seven-day cruise costs **3,500**，数字对不上，错。
   - B 说第三天可以在 Barbados 逛；行程表 Day Three 是 near **Haiti**，Barbados 是 Day Five，地点和时间错位，错。
   - C 说船能载约 600 名乘客；原文 ships carry over **2,000 passengers**，600 是 crew members（船员），把乘客和船员搞混，错。
   - D 说游客可以在加勒比海游览；原文 sail on cruises around the **Caribbean Sea**，正确。
3. 三个选项都有硬伤，选 D。

#### 例题 4-2：Jeremy Paxman 谈英国天气

**文章（P9，B）：**

> Bill Bryson, for example, concludes that, as the English weather is not at all exciting, the obsession with it can hardly be understood. He argues that "To an outsider, the most striking thing about the English weather is that there is not very much of it." Simply, the reason is that the unusual and unpredictable weather is almost unknown in the British Isles.
>
> Jeremy Paxman, however, disagrees with Bryson, arguing that the English weather is by nature attractive. "Bryson is wrong," he says, "because the English preference for the weather has nothing to do with the natural phenomena. The interest is less in the phenomena themselves, but in uncertainty." According to him, the weather in England is very changeable and uncertain and it attracts the English as well as the outsider.

**题目：**

> According to the passage, Jeremy Paxman believes that ______.

- A. Bill Bryson has little knowledge of the weather
- B. there is nothing special about the English weather
- C. the English weather attracts people to the British Isles
- D. English people talk about the weather for its uncertainty

**答案：D**

##### 讲题路径

1. 题目问 Jeremy Paxman 的观点，注意问的是 Paxman 而不是 Bryson，两人观点相反，先锁定 Paxman 那段。
2. 原文 Paxman 说 The interest is less in the phenomena themselves, but **in uncertainty**，又说天气 very changeable and uncertain，对应 D：人们谈论天气是因为它的不确定性。
3. 逐项排除：
   - A 说 Bryson 对天气一无所知；Paxman 只是不同意 Bryson 的观点，不等于对方一无所知，这是人身攻击、以偏概全；
   - B 说英国天气没什么特别，这其实是 Bryson 的看法（not very much of it），且 Paxman 认为它 by nature attractive，正好相反；
   - C 说天气把人吸引到不列颠群岛，原文是天气吸引 the English **as well as the outsider**，指引起兴趣，不是把人吸引来定居/到岛上，曲解了。
4. 因此选 D。

**重点短语：** has nothing to do with（与……无关）、preference（偏好）、uncertainty（不确定性）、changeable（多变的）、obsession（痴迷）。

#### 例题 4-3：开罗新出租车

**文章（C）：**

> According to officials, more than 11,000 brand new taxis, sporting trademark checked stripes on the sides, have already hit the streets of the capital, and they've been welcomed enthusiastically by passengers. Many people say that not only are the new taxis more comfortable, but they also make the streets of Cairo far more presentable. Furthermore, taxi fares have only increased by a small amount.
>
> The poor condition of most black and white taxis is almost legendary. Passengers often have to deal with windows and doors that do not open and close, and heavy, thick petrol gases. Air conditioning is unheard of. The new all-white taxis, meanwhile, are cleaner, more fuel-efficient and provide air conditioning on request. Furthermore, all the new taxis come equipped with functioning fare meters, which avoids arguments and sometimes fights with passengers!

**题目：**

> Which of the following statements is TRUE about the new taxis?

- A. They are much more presentable.
- B. Their passengers have to pay dramatically more.
- C. They are much more energy-saving.
- D. Only few of them provide air conditioning.

**答案：C**

##### 讲题路径

1. 问 TRUE，四选项逐一回原文定位。
2. 逐项核对：
   - A 说出租车更体面（presentable）；原文 make the **streets** of Cairo far more presentable，体面的是城市街道，不是出租车本身，偷换主体，错。
   - B 说乘客要付的钱大幅增加；原文 fares have only increased by a **small amount**，dramatically more 与 small amount 相反，错。
   - C 说新车更节能；原文 new all-white taxis ... more **fuel-efficient**（省油），energy-saving 与 fuel-efficient 同义，正确。
   - D 说几乎没有新车提供空调；原文新车 provide air conditioning on request，没空调是旧黑白出租车的问题（Air conditioning is unheard of），错。
3. 因此选 C。

**蒙题技巧（讲新事物的文章）：** 如果一篇文章讲新事物、考场上没时间细读，优先选**环境友好型**选项——环保、节能、低碳、不砍树等。这题选 energy-saving 正符合这个规律。

### 四种定位规则小结

顾老师在进入完整阅读前，把四种规则串起来回顾：

1. **题干意图不明确**：找小介词，to 找目的、for 找对象；
2. **题干关键词模糊**：按顺序对应回原文捋，同时简化选项、提炼关键词；
3. **选项信息不唯一（吃了吐）**：留意转折词和后半段总结，定位在前必错；
4. **选项需多次定位**：把四个选项分别在原文找到、逐一对比，务必看清题干问的是“正确”还是“不正确”。

她提醒：一定要看清楚问题——问选对的还是选错的、问正确还是不正确，再去找答案。

## 四、实战演练 A：护林员英雄故事（2020 山东一模）

四种定位规则讲完，顾老师让大家翻开讲义“实战演练”，先做 A 篇。这是 **2020 年山东地区一模考题**，本质是一篇**记叙文**，但带有少量议论成分，讲一个英雄的故事。后面一共四道题：两道细节题、一道推理判断题、一道主旨题。

老师发互动题，让学生“一键四连”，限时约 5 分钟独立完成，随后收卷逐题核对。

### 完整文章

**①**

> With bushfires continuing to swallow different parts of Australia, a local zoo in New South Wales — the state which is the easiest to catch fire — has hit the headlines with a heartwarming story.

**②**

> Right in the line of a bushfire, the keepers of Mogo Wildlife Park managed to protect all 200 animals from harm after they received evacuation orders, according to reports.

**③**

> Netizens were quick to react on social media and praised the bravery of the zoo keeper and staffs, as they stayed and managed to protect animals from uncontrolled fires and even sheltered some at their own house.

**④**

> The 40-year-old zoo keeper, Chad Staples, described the situation as the worst catastrophe, which felt like Armageddon (大决战) here. ...

**⑤**

> What's remarkable is that as the smaller ones needed extra shelter, Staples decided to take them to his own house.

**⑥**

> "Right now in my house there are animals of all descriptions in all the different rooms. They are safe and protected... not a single animal lost," he said.

**⑨**

> Located in the southern coast of New South Wales, Mogo Wildlife Park houses Australia's largest collection of primates (灵长类动物), also including different and unique animals such as zebras, giraffes, rhinos and red pandas.

（课件按题目需要引用段落，中间第⑦⑧段以省略号带过；以上为课件实际展示的全部段落原文。）

### 第 1 题（细节题）

> 1. New South Wales hits the headlines because of ______.

- A. its famous zoo
- B. its endangered animals
- C. the heartwarming event
- D. being easy to catch fire

**答案：C**

#### 讲题路径

1. 这是细节题，关键词 **because of**（因为），问新南威尔士为什么登上头条。
2. 回原文第①段：... has **hit the headlines with a heartwarming story**——它是因为一个暖心故事登上头条的，heartwarming event 与 heartwarming story 对应，选 C。
3. 这个故事的内容在第②段：护林员在接到 evacuation orders（撤退命令）后，managed to protect all 200 animals **from harm**（protect sb from sth，保护……免受……），本可撤退却留下保护约 200 只动物。
4. 逐一排除：
   - A 落脚在“动物园”，让它上头条的是这件事而不是动物园本身；
   - B 落脚在“濒危动物”，文章并未强调动物濒危；
   - D“容易着火”是这个州的属性，不是上头条的原因。
5. 这属于第一种“所见即所得”，看到什么选什么，选 C。

### 第 2 题（细节题，TRUE）

> 2. Which of the following is TRUE about Mogo Wildlife Park?

- A. It belongs to Chad Staples.
- B. None of the animals is missing.
- C. It merely holds rare animal species.
- D. Extra shelters were built for emergencies.

**答案：B**

#### 讲题路径

1. 这是细节题，问关于这个野生动物园哪一项正确，需要把各选项回原文核对。
2. A 说动物园属于 Chad Staples。第④段只说他是 **zoo keeper（饲养员）**，是员工不是老板，所以 It belongs to 不成立。
3. B 说没有动物失踪。第⑥段他本人说 **not a single animal lost**（一只动物都没少），与 none of the animals is missing 同义，正确。
4. C 说它只收容珍稀动物。第⑨段说 Mogo Wildlife Park **houses** Australia's largest collection of primates，also including different and unique animals such as zebras, giraffes, rhinos and red pandas——它保护大量灵长类和各种动物，many 并不珍稀，merely（仅仅）与原文不符。
5. D 说为紧急情况建了额外庇护所。第⑤段说 Staples decided to **take them to his own house**，他是把动物带回自己家，并没有额外建 shelter（庇护所），不成立。
6. A、C、D 都有硬伤，选 B。

### 第 3 题（推理判断题）

> 3. Which word can probably describe Chad Staples?

- A. Irresponsible. adj. 不负责任的
- B. Considerate. adj. 考虑周全的
- C. Selfish. adj. 自私的
- D. Energetic. adj. 精力充沛的

**答案：B**

#### 讲题路径

1. 这是推理判断题，问哪个词最能描述这位管理员。
2. 即使不读原文，从他让小动物住回自己家、不让它们流落街头或死去，也能看出他有奉献精神、大公无私。
3. A 的 irresponsible（不负责任）：ir- 是否定前缀，去掉 ir 换成 responsible 才对，正好说反了。
4. B 的 considerate（考虑周到的）：consider 是“考虑”，-ate 表示性质，指这个人具备想得多、想得周全的性质，符合。
5. C 的 selfish（自私）：如果自私他就只会想自己、不会保护动物，也说反了。
6. D 的 energetic（精力充沛）：原文体现不出来，他也很累、也需要休息；选它属于主观臆断。原文没提到的就不能自己瞎选。
7. A、C 说反，D 无依据，只能选 B。

### 第 4 题（主旨题，best title）

> 4. Which of the following is the best title for the passage?

- A. The Heroic Job
- B. The Horrific Fire
- C. The Extinct Animals
- D. The Brave Zoo Keeper

**答案：D**

#### 讲题路径

1. 这是主旨题，问最佳标题，先判断文章主要写什么——介绍一个动物管理员，是写人。
2. A“The Heroic Job（英雄般的工作）”：重点不是这份工作，而是这个人，不是每个管理员都能做到这一步；落脚点在“工作”，模糊、指代不明。
3. B“The Horrific Fire（恐怖的大火）”：大火确实恐怖，但写作对象不是火，而是这个管理员，落脚点在“大火”。
4. C“The Extinct Animals（濒危动物）”：搞错了重点，落脚点在“动物”。
5. D“The Brave Zoo Keeper（勇敢的动物管理员）”直接把文章所写的人作为标题，与本文贴合度最高。
6. A、B、C 落脚点分别是工作、大火、动物，都偏了；文章写人，选 D。

### 本篇生词（延伸拓展，含音标）

| 序号 | 词汇和短语 | 音标 | 词性和释义 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | swallow | /ˈswɒləʊ/ | v. 吞没；吞咽 |
| 2 | netizen | /ˈnetɪzn/ | n. 网民 |
| 3 | bravery | /ˈbreɪvəri/ | n. 勇气；勇敢 |
| 4 | precise | /prɪˈsaɪs/ | adj. 准确的 |
| 5 | remarkable | /rɪˈmɑːkəbl/ | adj. 引人注目的；显著的 |
| 6 | highlight | /ˈhaɪlaɪt/ | v. 突出；强调 |
| 7 | house | /haʊs/ | v. 给……提供住所；放置 |
| 8 | hit the headline | — | 成为新闻头条 |

顾老师逐个讲解：

1. **swallow（吞咽）**：既可以接具体的东西（吞食物），也可以接不太好的抽象名词——把悲伤、难过、委屈“吞了下去”，都能用 swallow。
2. **netizen（网民）**：前面是 net（网），对应“市民”citizen，把前面换成 city 即可。
3. **bravery（勇气）**：名词，对应形容词 brave。
4. **precise（准确的）**：注意读音，一般用来形容数字、时间，表示精确、精准。
5. **remarkable（显著的）**：相当于 outstanding、fabulous、excellent，是褒义、表示很厉害。
6. **highlight（突出；强调）**：high（高）+ light（光），即“高光”，给你标亮、加标注，表强调。
7. **house（提供住所）**：名词动用，作名词是房子，作动词是给……提供房子/住所。
8. **hit the headline（成为新闻头条）**：hit 在此是动作；hit 还可作形容词，如 hit song（热门歌曲）。

### 长难句：with 的复合结构

顾老师重点讲 with 的复合结构，预告这是**秋季班第一讲**的内容。

**结构（黑板手写）：**

> **【手写】** with + n. + to do / doing / done / 介词短语 / adj.

她逐一举例：

1. **to do**：With a lot of homework to do, I have no time to play (with you).（有很多作业要做，我没时间玩）
2. **doing**：With bushfires continuing to swallow different parts of Australia ...（大火持续吞噬澳大利亚多地）
3. **done**：With my homework done, let's play outside.（作业写完了，出去玩吧）
4. **介词短语**：With a book under her arm, our teacher came into the classroom.（老师腋下夹着一本书走进教室）
5. **形容词**：With the sky blue, let's play outside.（天空真蓝，出去玩吧）

她强调：with 是**独立结构**，独立于主句，不影响主句的结构。

**延伸拓展（划句子主干）：** 课件让学生划出原句主干：

> (With bushfires continuing to swallow different parts of Australia), a local zoo in New South Wales — the state (which is the easiest to catch fire) — has hit the headlines with a heartwarming story.

逐部分标注：

- with 的复合结构：With bushfires continuing ...
- 主语：**local zoo**
- 状语：in New South Wales；with a heartwarming story
- 定语从句：which is the easiest to catch fire
- 插入语：the state ... 用来解释 New South Wales
- 谓语：**has hit**
- 宾语：**the headlines**

她还指出，后面另一个 with（with a heartwarming story）并不是 with 复合结构，只是普通介词短语作状语；这个长句砍瓜切菜后，主干就是 **local zoo（主语）+ has hit（谓语）+ the headlines（宾语）**。

#### 另一个长单句（三重从句）

> What's remarkable is that as the smaller ones needed extra shelter, Staples decided to take them to his own house.

顾老师带学生数从句：

1. **What's remarkable** 在句首，是**主语从句**；
2. **that ...** 跟在系动词后面，是**表语从句**；
3. 表语从句里的 **as the smaller ones needed extra shelter** 有自己的主谓宾（the smaller ones 主语 + needed 谓语 + extra shelter 宾语），as 引导**原因状语从句**，意为“因为这些小个的需要额外庇护”。

所以这个句子一共三重从句：主从 + 表从，表从里又套一个（原因）状从。

## 六、实战演练 B：汽车与自行车（说明文）

第二篇文章比 A 篇稍长，是一篇**说明文**。故事性不强，但里面有几个长难句，读起来相对费劲。同样是四道题，老师收卷后逐题核对并拆解长难句。

### 完整文章

**①**

> There is a range of activities that require movements of about one to four or five miles. These might be leisure activities, such as moving from home to swimming pool, tennis club, the theater or other cultural centers, or to a secondary or more advanced school; or they might be movements associated with work and shopping in the central areas of cities. The use of cars which are able to carry five people at 80 mph for satisfying these needs is wasteful of space and most productive of disturbance to other road users.

**②**

> The use of the bicycle, or some more modern derivative (派生的) of it, is probably worth more consideration than what has recently been given to it. The bicycle itself is a remarkably efficient and simple device for using human muscular energy for transportation. In pure energy terms, it is four to five times as efficient as walking, even though human walking itself is twice as efficient as the movement of effective animals such as dogs. It is still widely used, not only in some developing countries where bicycles are major means of people and goods, but in a few richer towns such as Amsterdam in Holland and Cambridge in England.

**③**

> The bicycle neither gives enough protection from the weather nor is very suitable for carrying goods, and demands considerable muscular work to make progress against wind or uphill. It also offers its rider no protection against collisions with other vehicles. All these difficulties could, however, be greatly got rid of with relatively small changes in design. The whole machine could be enclosed in a plastic bubble (气泡) which would provide some protection in case of accidents.

### 第 1 题（段落大意，细节题）

> 1. The main idea of the first paragraph is that the car ______.

- A. can satisfy the demand for speed
- B. causes waste of space
- C. produces disturbance to other road users
- D. is far from perfect for short range movements

**答案：D**

#### 讲题路径

1. 问第一段的大意，这种“问某一段”的题要分情况：这题已具体到 the car 怎么样，属于细节题而不是主旨题。
2. 第①段先讲有一系列需要出行 1 到 4、5 英里的活动，可能是 leisure activities（休闲活动），也可能是工作、购物相关的出行。
3. 最后说 The use of cars ... is **wasteful of space** and **most productive of disturbance** to other road users——汽车既浪费空间，又对其他道路使用者造成困扰。
4. B、C 单独看都对，但都只说了一半、不全面；D“is far from perfect for short range movements（对短距离出行而言远不完美）”把两点都概括进去，最完整。
5. 先说汽车不好，其实是立个靶子，由此引出下文“自行车更好”。选 D。

### 第 2 题（细节题）

> 2. More attention should be given to the bicycle as a means of transport because it is ______.

- A. a very efficient and simple device
- B. much cheaper than a car
- C. widely used in Amsterdam and Cambridge
- D. still used by rich people

**答案：A**

#### 讲题路径

1. 问为什么应该更关注自行车，回第②段找原因。
2. 原文 The bicycle itself is a remarkably **efficient and simple device** for using human muscular energy for transportation——自行车是利用人体肌肉能量、非常高效且简单的工具，直接对应 A。
3. 排除：
   - B“比汽车便宜”原文没提；
   - C“在阿姆斯特丹和剑桥被广泛使用”是用来佐证它仍被使用的例子，不是“该被关注”的根本原因，答非所问；
   - D“仍被富人使用”与原文相反，那两个地方是 richer towns，但自行车并非富人专用。
4. 选 A。

### 第 3 题（细节题）

> 3. Enclosing the bicycle in a plastic bubble would ______.

- A. make it easier to use
- B. save muscular energy
- C. provide protection from the weather
- D. prevent it from causing serious injuries

**答案：D**

#### 讲题路径

1. 问把自行车包进塑料气泡（plastic bubble）是为了什么，回第③段。
2. 原文 The whole machine could be enclosed in a plastic bubble which would provide some protection **in case of accidents**——它在发生事故时提供保护，即防止造成严重伤害，对应 D。
3. 排除：
   - A“让它更好用”、B“节省肌肉能量”都不是包气泡的目的；
   - C“防天气”：前面说自行车 gives ... protection from the weather 不足，但气泡针对的是 accidents（碰撞事故），不是天气。
4. 选 D。本句中 **enclose** 意为“包裹、围住”（be enclosed in，被包裹在……里）。

### 第 4 题（细节题，UNTRUE）

> 4. Which of the following statements is UNTRUE for the present bicycle?

- A. It is far more efficient than the movement of animals.
- B. It offers its rider no protection.
- C. It is not very suitable for carrying goods.
- D. It can hardly be improved on.

**答案：D**

#### 讲题路径

1. 题干 **UNTRUE**，要选关于当前自行车“不正确”的一项。
2. A：第②段说自行车效率是步行的 4 到 5 倍，而步行又是狗等动物运动效率的 2 倍，所以它比动物运动高效，符合原文。
3. B：第③段说 It also offers its rider no protection against collisions，确实不能给骑手提供（碰撞）保护，符合原文。
4. C：第③段 nor is very suitable for carrying goods，不太适合载货，符合原文。
5. D：“It can hardly be improved on（几乎没有改进空间）”与原文相反——All these difficulties could ... be greatly got rid of with **relatively small changes in design**，只要设计上小小的改变就能克服这些问题，说明还有很大改进空间。
6. 老师补充：不同地区、不同种族的人身体条件和骑车习惯不同（如北欧人腿长、车轮更大、握把方式不同），都要据此改造，这本身就是改进空间。
7. A、B、C 都符合原文，D 错误，选 D。

### 本篇生词（延伸拓展，含音标）

| 序号 | 词汇和短语 | 音标 | 词性和释义 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | efficient | /ɪˈfɪʃnt/ | adj. 高效的 |
| 2 | effective | /ɪˈfektɪv/ | adj. 有效的；生效的 |
| 3 | means | /miːnz/ | n. 方法；手段 |
| 4 | consideration | /kənˌsɪdəˈreɪʃn/ | n. 仔细考虑；体贴 |
| 5 | considerable | /kənˈsɪdərəbl/ | adj. 相当多的 |
| 6 | employ | /ɪmˈplɔɪ/ | v. 利用；雇佣 |
| 7 | be associated with | — | 与……有关 |
| 8 | a range/variety of | — | 不同种类的 |

顾老师重点辨析：

1. **efficient（高效的）**：名词是 **efficiency**（效率）。
2. **effective（有效的）**：与 efficient 形近，注意区分。
3. **means（方法；手段）**：名词；去掉 s 的 mean 是形容词，意为“刻薄的、尖刻的”（如 Taylor Swift 的 Mean，why you gotta be so mean）。
4. **consideration**：动词 consider 对应的名词。
5. **considerable（相当多的）**：与前面 A 篇考过的 **considerate（体贴的、考虑周到的）**完全不同。considerate 是“考虑得多 → 周到、体贴”；considerable 是“值得被思考 → 要么多、要么大”，译为“可观的”，指数量多或体积大。
6. **employ**：意为“雇佣”，也可表“使用（某物）”。
7. **be associated with**：与……有关系。
8. **a range / variety of**：相当于 all kinds of，各种各样的。

### 名词加 s 专题（延伸拓展）

顾老师借 means 总结“不是所有加 s 的都是复数”，分三类：

**1. 部分不可数名词以 s 结尾：**

> athletics (体育运动), glass, grass, gas, progress; maths, politics, physics, statistics (统计学), economics (经济学)

这些虽然以 s 结尾，却是不可数名词，不是复数形式。

**2. 一些名词只有复数形式：**

> news, means, customs (海关，关税)

它们的单数形式意思不同：new（mean 是形容词“刻薄”）、custom（单数指风俗习惯，不指海关）。

**3. 一些名词的某个释义通常以复数形式出现：**

| 单词 | 单数义 | 复数义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| accommodation | A. 住所 | accommodations：B. 膳食 |
| good | B. 正直的行为；善行（do some good） | goods：A. 商品，货品 |
| manner | B. 方式，方法；举止，态度 | manners：A. 礼貌，礼仪（table manners） |
| saving | B. 节省，节约 | savings：A. 储蓄，存款 |
| possession / belonging | possession：A. 拥有；belonging：B. 归属感 | possessions / belongings：C. 个人财务 |

其中 possession 的两个易混结构（黑板手写）：

> **【手写】** sb. be in possession of sth.（某人拥有某物）
> **【手写】** sth. be in the possession of sb.（某物被某人拥有）

- 人在前、物在后：somebody is in possession of something；
- 物在前、人在后：something is in the possession of somebody；
- 用法类似之前讲过的 charge。

### 本篇长难句（划主干）

**长难句 1（第①段，主系表）：**

> The use of cars (which are able to carry five people at 80 mph for satisfying these needs) is wasteful of space and most productive of disturbance to other road users.

- 主语：The use of cars
- 中间 which ... 是定语从句，先不管
- 系动词：is
- 表语：wasteful of space and most productive of disturbance ...
- 主干是主系表。

**长难句 2（第②段）：**

> The use of the bicycle, or some more modern derivative of it, is probably worth more consideration than (what has recently been given to it).

- 主语：The use of the bicycle
- 插入语：or some more modern derivative of it
- 系动词：is
- 表语：worth more consideration
- what has recently been given to it 是宾语从句
- 句意：自行车，或其他现代的衍生交通工具，值得比现在更多的关注。

她总结方法：插入成分、从句都是修饰，可以先不管，直接找到主系表或主谓宾，句子就清楚了。

## 七、词汇精讲：五个重点单词

阅读部分结束，进入最后一趴——词汇精讲。今天讲五个单词，大多数来自**选修六**。

### 单词 1：distinguish

**distinguish /dɪˈstɪŋɡwɪʃ/**

- 教材与考情：人教版选择性必修一、外研版必修三、近三年高考词频 4
- **【手写】** tell apart；outstanding

**1. v. 区分；辨别**

搭配：**distinguish A from B / distinguish between A and B**

- There is something about music that distinguishes it from all other art forms.
- People who can't distinguish between colors are said to be colorblind.

**2. v. 使有别于；使出众**

- In the interview, something creative should be done by graduates to distinguish themselves from other interviewers.

顾老师讲记忆法：dis- 表否定，后面 sting 可联想“鬼”，“不听鬼说”——自己有辨别能力，所以 distinguish 是区分、辨别，等于 tell sth apart。

**词汇拓展：**

1. **distinct /dɪˈstɪŋkt/ adj. 清晰的、明白的；有区别的**
   - Salvador Dali used to use many artistic media, but they all have a distinct Dali style.
   - 她借画家举例：达利（流淌的时钟）、莫奈（印象派、颜色过渡不明显）、毕加索（抽象、多只眼睛），都有明显可辨的 distinct style。
2. **distinction /dɪˈstɪŋkʃn/ n. 区别、区分；特质、特点**
   - **【手写】** character
   - The London Underground has the distinction of being the oldest and most complex underground system in the world.
   - 能区别于别人的，就是自己的特点，约等于 character；相应地 distinct 约等于 characteristic。
3. **distinguishable adj. 可区别的、可分辨的**
   - 例如双胞胎中一个脸上长了一颗痣，两人就 distinguishable、可以被区别开来。

**真题复现（P17）：**

> The lecture mainly deals with the trouble (young children have) ______ right from wrong.

- A. distinguished
- B. to distinguish
- C. distinguishing
- D. to be distinguished

**答案：C**

**【手写】** have trouble (in) doing；定语从句

讲题：括号里 young children have 是修饰 trouble 的**定语从句**，还原成正常语序是 have trouble **(in) doing** sth（in 被省略，剩下 doing），所以选 distinguishing。她提醒：遇到这种后置定语，不妨把定语从句前置到所修饰名词前再判断，就不会只盯着单个形容词、被“令人……/感到……”干扰。

**写作应用：** 图表作文常需比较信息，可写 how to distinguish A from B，用于场景介绍。

### 单词 2：motivate

**motivate /ˈməʊtɪveɪt/ v. 推动；激励；激发**

- 教材与考情：人教版选择性必修二、外研版选择性必修二、近三年高考词频 6
- **【手写】** mov-；inspire

搭配：**motivate sb to do sth**

- The May Fourth Movement was launched in 1919 and its spirit has been motivating Chinese youth to make contributions to national rejuvenation ever since.

顾老师讲词根：mot 和 mov 同根，表“移动”，-ate 是常见动词后缀；motivate 就是“让你动”，给你推动力。她特别强调 motivate 更强调**已经产生了行动力**，并与几个近义词语辨析：

- **inspire**：启发灵感、激发斗志，你还停留在原地、只是想法被点燃；如果开始努力学习、动起来了，才叫 motivate。
- **urge**：内心的一种欲望。
- **stimulate**：更强调“刺激”，刺激不一定是好的，也可能是负面的（如一句话伤到了人，也是一种 stimulation）。

名词：**motivation n. 动力**，抽象名词、不可数，且一定是好的、正面的才用 motivation。

**真题复现：**

> It is thought that ______ (motivate) is the leading factor contributing to language learning.

**答案：motivation**（去 e 加 -ion）

一个人的动力、内在动机是促成语言学习的重要因素。

### 单词 3：submit

**submit /səbˈmɪt/**

- 教材与考情：人教版选择性必修三、北师大版选择性必修三、外研版选择性必修四、近三年高考词频 5
- **【手写】** miss（词根）

**1. v. 提交**

搭配：**submit sth to sb**

- I approve of your submitting your article to the press now.

**2. v. 顺从；屈服**

搭配：**submit to sb/sth**，相当于 give in to

- Employees who refuse to submit to the company's regulations will be dismissed.

顾老师讲记忆：sub- 表“在……下面”（如 subway 地铁），mit 和 miss 同根、表“传送”；在下面往上传送，像古代大臣给皇帝递东西，引申为“顺从、屈服”，也指下级对上级“提交”。

**词汇拓展：**

- **submission /səbˈmɪʃn/ n. 屈服、投降；提交、呈递**
  - This year's English movie dubbing competition will be held, but no date has yet been set for the submission of the entries to be selected.
- **submissive adj. 顺从的、听话的**（如 a submissive cat，听话的小猫咪）。这两个词很少考，知道即可。

**真题复现：**

> The sales manager has decided to postpone ______ (submit) his plan to the board.

**答案：submitting**

讲题：postpone（推迟）和 delay 一样，只能接**动名词**作宾语，即 postpone doing = delay doing；submit 要**双写 t 再加 ing**，所以是 submitting。

### 单词 4：qualify / qualification

**qualify /ˈkwɒlɪfaɪ/**（**【手写】** -fy）

**1. v. 取得资格；合格**

搭配：**qualify for sth**

- He's been informed that he doesn't qualify for the scholarship because of his academic background.

**2. v. 使合格；使具备资格**

搭配：**qualify sb for sth**

- This training course will qualify you for a better job.

**词汇拓展：**

1. **qualified /ˈkwɒlɪfaɪd/ adj. 符合资格的；具备……的资历**

   搭配：**be qualified for sth / be qualified to do sth**

   - A man without previous experience is not qualified for the job.
   - I don't know much about it, so I don't feel qualified to comment.

2. **quality /ˈkwɒləti/ n. 质量**

   - In order to compete with others, a factory should aim at quality rather than quantity.
   - 搭配：be of good/poor quality（质量好/差）；Books of good quality enrich the mind.

3. **quantity n. 数量**

   顾老师把 quality 和 quantity 放一起对比：quantity 里有“筐”的音，拿筐装的肯定是数量多，所以 quantity 是数量，quality 是质量。

**真题复现（P21）：**

> She recognized that she was not ______ (qualify) for the post.

**答案：qualified**

讲题：be qualified for 是固定搭配，qualify 要把 **y 变 i 再加 ed**，即 qualified。

**写作应用：** 申请信常用 I think that I am qualified for the job（我认为自己有资格胜任这份工作）。

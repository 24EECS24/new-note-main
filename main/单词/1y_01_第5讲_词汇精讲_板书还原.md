## 1. addict　/ˈædɪkt/　〔近三年高考词频 5〕

**n. 上瘾者，对……入迷的人**
- She is a golf addict and plays as much as she can.

**词汇拓展：**

**(1) addicted**　/əˈdɪktɪd/　**adj. 成瘾的；入迷的；沉溺于**
- He is addicted **to** basketball.（介词用 **to**）

**(2) addiction**　/əˈdɪkʃn/　**n. 上瘾**
- Some weight problems are caused by an addiction to sugar and fat.

---

## 2. apply　/əˈplaɪ/　〔近三年高考词频 14〕

**【手写】apply for 申请；apply to 向……（申请）**

**(1) v.（通常以书面形式）申请，请求**
- I have applied **for** a passport.
- I applied **to** the school **for** a scholarship.（向公司 / 大学申请）

**(2) v. 应用，运用**
- Scientific discoveries are often applied **to** industrial production methods.

**(3) v. 适用于**
- That argument does not apply in this case.

## 3. appreciate　/əˈpriːʃieɪt/　〔近三年高考词频 19〕

**(1) v. 欣赏，重视；感谢，感激**
- We would appreciate you letting us know of any problems.
- I would appreciate it if you could inform me of the arrangement.

**(2) v. 理解，明白**
- We appreciate that caring for children is an important job.

**词汇拓展：**

**(1) appreciation**　/əˌpriːʃiˈeɪʃn/　**n. 欣赏；感激；理解**
- This gift is in appreciation of all that you've done for us.

**(2) appreciative**　/əˈpriːʃətɪv/　**adj. 感激的；欣赏的**
- The company was very appreciative of my efforts.

**【手写】写作句子：** I'm writing to express my heartfelt appreciation.

> 敲黑板·写作应用：请你写信**申请**做冬奥会的志愿者（第四季度课程：申请信的框架和表达）。

## 4. arise　/əˈraɪz/　vi.　〔近三年高考词频 5〕

**v. 产生，由……引起，出现**
- 不规则变化：**arise – arose – arisen**
- A new challenge has arisen.
- Accidents often arise **from** carelessness.

**敲黑板：arise 为不及物动词，没有被动语态。**

**【手写】易混四词辨析：**

| 单词 | 词性 | 含义 | 不规则变化 |
|---|---|---|---|
| rise | vi. | 上升 | rose, risen |
| raise | vt. | +sb 抚养 / +sth 举起 / +钱 筹集 | raised, raised |
| arise | vi. | 产生，出现 | arose, arisen |
| arouse | vt. | 唤起 | aroused, aroused |

## 5. focus　/ˈfəʊkəs/　〔近三年高考词频 54〕

**n. 中心点，焦点**
**v. 集中注意力于……；聚焦于……**

- His comments provided a focus for debate.
- Today we are going to focus **on** the question of homeless people in London.

**词汇拓展：全神贯注于**
- focus **on** / concentrate **on** / fix **on** / pay attention **to**
- In this lecture we will concentrate on these important words.
- I didn't pay attention to what the teacher was saying.
- Jack tried to fix his mind on the job at hand.

## 6. prefer　/prɪˈfɜː(r)/　〔近三年高考词频 84〕

**v. 更喜欢……**
- I prefer red to blue.
- I prefer not to think about it.
- He prefers making speeches after careful preparation.

**搭配总结：prefer A to B；prefer to do / doing**

- He preferred to stay at home rather than go with us.
- I would prefer it if you didn't tell other people.
- I would prefer that you did not mention my name.

**搭配总结：prefer to do A rather than do B / prefer it if / prefer that**

**【手写】** Rather than **take** a bus, he prefers **to take** a bike to school.

**词汇拓展：**

**preference**　/ˈprefrəns/　**n. 偏爱**
- It's a matter of personal preference.
- （补充—19 年 1 月上海完形）Local governments can foster this sustainable activity by giving preference **to** responsible operators and even high-paying consumers.

## 7. schedule　/ˈʃedjuːl/（英）　/ˈskedʒuːl/（美）　〔近三年高考词频 50〕

**n. & v. 计划；安排**
**n. 日程表**

**【手写】timetable；be due to**

- Our aircraft arrived **on** schedule.　**如期**
- We arrived two hours **ahead of** schedule.　**比预期提前**
- The project has already fallen **behind** schedule.　**比预期落后**
- The space shuttle **is scheduled to** blast off at 04:38.　**按计划应该**

**敲黑板·写作应用（邀请外教 Tom 参加周六友谊联赛）：**

## 10. various　/ˈveəriəs/　〔近三年高考词频 6〕

**adj. 各种各样的，多姿多彩的**
- There are various ways of learning English.

**词汇拓展：**

**(1) variety**　/vəˈraɪəti/
- ① **n. 不同种类，多种式样**：This tool can be used in **a variety of** ways.
- ② **n. 多样性（= diversity）**：We all need variety in our diet.

**(2) vary**　/ˈveəri/　**v.（大小、形状等）相异，不同；变更，改变**
- The students' work varies considerably in quality.
- Prices vary according to the type of room you require.

**【手写】**
- vary **between** A and B；vary **from** A to B
- 同义：change / turn / go / alter
- 注意区分 **vary** 与 **very**

# III. 必修一高频词根词缀

## 词缀汇总（10 词）

| 序号 | 单词 | 序号 | 单词 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **dis**like | 2 | **dis**advantage |
| 3 | **dis**agree | 4 | **un**fair |
| 5 | **re**public | 6 | **re**cognize |
| 7 | **pre**fer | 8 | **re**ward |
| 9 | **en**rich | 10 | **en**able |

**前缀归纳：**
- **dis-**：dislike, disadvantage, disagree（表否定、相反）
- **un-**：unfair（表否定）
- **re-**：republic, recognize, reward
- **pre-**：prefer（在……之前）
- **en-**：enrich, enable（使……）

**【手写】**
- return, review（re- 再、又）
- re- 向前；pro-
- enrich：使……富裕；enable：使……能够

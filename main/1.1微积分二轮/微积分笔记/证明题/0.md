## 函数最值与单调性的关系
最值取在定义域端点 $\iff$ 函数单调
最值不取在定义域端点 $\iff$ 函数不单调
最值点取在极值点处

图1：严格单调递增函数在闭区间上的示意图，最值仅取在区间两个端点处
```tikz
\begin{document}
\definecolor{curveblue}{rgb}{0.2,0.3,0.8}
\begin{tikzpicture}[x=1cm,y=1cm,>=stealth,scale=1.2]
    \draw[->] (-0.3,0) -- (1.5,0) node[right,font=\small] {$x$};
    \draw[->] (0,-0.3) -- (0,1.5) node[above,font=\small] {$y$};
    \node[below left,font=\small] at (0,0) {$O$};
    \draw[thick,curveblue,domain=0:1] plot(\x,\x);
    \fill (0,0) circle (1.2pt) node[below left,font=\small] {$(a,f(a))$};
    \fill (1,1) circle (1.2pt) node[above right,font=\small] {$(b,f(b))$};
    \draw[gray,dashed] (0,0) -- (0,-0.15);
    \draw[gray,dashed] (1,0) -- (1,-0.15);
    \node[below,font=\small] at (0,-0.15) {$a$};
    \node[below,font=\small] at (1,-0.15) {$b$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

图2：不单调函数（以$f(x)=|x|$在$[-1,1]$上为例）的示意图，最小值取在内部极值点处，最大值取在区间端点
```tikz
\begin{document}
\definecolor{curveblue}{rgb}{0.2,0.3,0.8}
\definecolor{tangentred}{rgb}{0.8,0.2,0.2}
\begin{tikzpicture}[x=1cm,y=1cm,>=stealth,scale=1.2]
    \draw[->] (-1.5,0) -- (1.5,0) node[right,font=\small] {$x$};
    \draw[->] (0,-0.3) -- (0,1.5) node[above,font=\small] {$y$};
    \node[below left,font=\small] at (0,0) {$O$};
    \draw[thick,curveblue,domain=-1:0] plot(\x,{-\x});
    \draw[thick,curveblue,domain=0:1] plot(\x,\x);
    \fill[tangentred] (0,0) circle (1.2pt) node[below right,font=\small] {$(c,f(c))$};
    \fill (-1,1) circle (1.2pt) node[above left,font=\small] {$(a,f(a))$};
    \fill (1,1) circle (1.2pt) node[above right,font=\small] {$(b,f(b))$};
    \draw[gray,dashed] (-1,0) -- (-1,-0.15);
    \draw[gray,dashed] (1,0) -- (1,-0.15);
    \node[below,font=\small] at (-1,-0.15) {$a$};
    \node[below,font=\small] at (1,-0.15) {$b$};
    \node[below,font=\small] at (0,-0.15) {$c$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

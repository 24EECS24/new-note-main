**反双曲正弦函数** $\displaystyle y=\ln(x+\sqrt{1+x^2})$ 
是**奇函数** 
反函数是**双曲正弦** $\sinh x$ 
$\displaystyle y'=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}$ 

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,line cap=round,line join=round,scale=1]
\path (-6.6,-3.3) rectangle (6.6,3.3); %fbbox
\clip (-6.6,-3.3) rectangle (6.6,3.3); %clip
\draw[->] (-6,0) -- (6,0); %gen
\draw[->] (0,-3) -- (0,3); %gen
\draw[thick,domain=-6:6,samples=64] plot(\x,{ln(\x+sqrt(1+(\x)^2))}); %gen
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```



**双曲正切**$\dfrac{e^x-1}{e^x+1}$ 
是**奇函数** 

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,line cap=round,line join=round,scale=1]
\path (-2.72,-1.1) rectangle (2.23,1.1); %fbbox
\clip (-2.72,-1.1) rectangle (2.23,1.1); %clip
\draw[->] (-2.5,0) -- (2,0); %gen
\draw[->] (0,-1) -- (0,1); %gen
\draw[thick,domain=-2.5:2,samples=64] plot(\x,{(exp(\x)-1)/(exp(\x)+1)}); %gen
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```


**双曲正弦函数**$\displaystyle y=\frac{e^x - e^{-x}}{2}$，
是奇函数。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,line cap=round,line join=round,scale=1]
\path (-2.72,-8.12) rectangle (2.23,5.63); %fbbox
\clip (-2.72,-8.12) rectangle (2.23,5.63); %clip
\draw[->] (-2.5,0) -- (2,0); %gen
\draw[->] (0,-7.5) -- (0,5); %gen
\draw[thick,domain=-2.5:2,samples=64] plot(\x,{(exp(\x) - exp(-\x))/(2)}); %gen
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

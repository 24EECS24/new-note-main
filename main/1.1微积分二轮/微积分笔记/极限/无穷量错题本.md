## 习题1.19
设$x \to 0$时，$e^{\tan x}-e^{\sin x}$与$x^n$是同阶无穷小，则$n=\underline{\quad3\quad}$ 

`````col
````col-md
flexGrow=1.3
===

$$
\begin{align*}
解:&\\[4pt]
&\displaystyle \lim\limits_{x \to x_0}\frac{e^{\tan x}-e^{\sin x}}{x^n} \\[4pt]
&=\displaystyle \lim\limits_{x \to x_0}\frac{e^{\sin x}\left(e^{\tan x - \sin x} - 1\right)}{x^n} \\[4pt]
&=\displaystyle \lim\limits_{x \to x_0}\frac{e^{\sin x}(\tan x - \sin x)}{x^n} \\[4pt]
&=\displaystyle \lim\limits_{x \to x_0}\frac{e^{\sin x} \cdot \tan x \cdot (1-\cos x)}{x^n} \\[4pt]
&=\displaystyle \lim\limits_{x \to x_0}\frac{x \cdot \frac{1}{2}x^2}{x^n}\\[4pt]
&因此n=3。\\[4pt]

解析:&\\[4pt]
&当x \to 0时：\\[4pt]
&\tan x \to 0，e^{\tan x} \to 1\\[4pt]
&\sin x \to 0，e^{\sin x} \to 1\\[4pt]
&\because当u \to 0时，e^u - 1 \sim u\\[4pt]
&\therefore e^{\tan x} - e^{\sin x} =e^{\sin x}\left(e^{\tan x - \sin x} - 1\right)\\[4pt]
&\because当x \to 0时\tan x - \sin x \to 0，\\[4pt]
&\therefore e^{\tan x - \sin x} -1  \sim \tan x - \sin x\\[4pt]
&\therefore e^{\sin x}\left(e^{\tan x - \sin x} -1\right)  \sim  e^{\sin x}(\tan x - \sin x) \\[4pt]
&\therefore \displaystyle \lim\limits_{x \to x_0}\frac{e^{\tan x}-e^{\sin x}}{x^n} \\[4pt]
&=\displaystyle \lim\limits_{x \to x_0}\frac{e^{\sin x}(\tan x - \sin x)}{x^n} \\[4pt]
\end{align*}
$$

````col-md

===

$$
\begin{align*}
&\because 含x^n的有\frac{1}{2}x^2 \sim 1-\cos x\\[4pt]
&\therefore \tan x - \sin x \\[4pt]
&=\tan x - \left( \frac{\sin x}{\cos x} \cdot \cos x \right) \\[4pt]
&=\tan x - \tan x \cdot \cos x \\[4pt]
&= \tan x(1-\cos x)\\[4pt]
&\therefore \displaystyle \lim\limits_{x \to x_0}\frac{e^{\sin x}(\tan x - \sin x)}{x^n} \\[4pt]
&=\displaystyle \lim\limits_{x \to x_0}\frac{e^{\sin x} \cdot \tan x \cdot (1-\cos x)}{x^n} \\[4pt]
&\because x \to 0时\\[4pt]
&\frac{1}{2}x^2 \sim 1-\cos x，\tan x \sim x，e^{\sin x} \sim 1\\[4pt]
&\therefore \displaystyle \lim\limits_{x \to x_0}\frac{e^{\sin x} \cdot \tan x \cdot (1-\cos x)}{x^n} \\[4pt]
&=\displaystyle \lim\limits_{x \to x_0}\frac{x \cdot \frac{1}{2}x^2}{x^n}\\[4pt]
&即e^{\tan x} - e^{\sin x} \sim \frac{1}{2}x^3，因此n=3。\\[4pt]
\end{align*}
$$
`````

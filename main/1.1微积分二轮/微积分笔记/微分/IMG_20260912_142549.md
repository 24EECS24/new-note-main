## 利用导数定义求极限
$$
\begin{align*}
\because f'(0) &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x - 0} \\
&= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(3x) - f(0)}{3x - 0} \\
\because f(0) &= 0, \\
\therefore f'(0) &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(3x)}{3x} = -1\\
\therefore \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(3x)}{x} \\
&= 3\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(3x)}{3x} \\
&= -3
\end{align*}
$$

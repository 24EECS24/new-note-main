### 版面识别
对 $y$ 轴方向同理：用平行于 $y$ 轴方向的平面 $\beta$ 截曲面，$\lim_{\Delta y\to0}[f(x_0,y_0+\Delta y)-f(x_0,y_0)]/\Delta y$ 为对 $y$ 轴方向变化率，记 $f_y'(x_0,y_0)$、$\partial f/\partial y|$、$z_y'$。注：若 $z=f(x,y)$ 在点处 $x,y$ 轴方向偏导都存在，称该点有偏导数；若区域 $D$ 上每点都有偏导则存在偏导函数 $\partial f/\partial x,\partial f/\partial y$（$f_x',f_y'$），无论对哪个轴方向求导都可，具体求法见例题。含一张 3D 截面图。

图：3D 曲面被平行于 $yOz$ 的竖直平面 $\beta$ 截出抛物线（蓝），其切线（红）斜率即 $f_y$；底面标出 $(x_0,y_0)$、$(x_0,y_0+\Delta y)$ 与 $\Delta y$。

\begin{document}
\definecolor{curveblue}{rgb}{0.2,0.3,0.8}

\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\scriptsize]
\def\H{2}\def\K{0.16}
% beta plane fixed x=0 -> screen plane spanned by y (horizontal) and z (vertical)

\foreach \yy in {-1.9,-0.95,0,0.95,1.9}{

  plot({-0.85*\t+\yy},{-0.5*\t+\H-\K*(\t*\t+\yy*\yy)});}
\foreach \xx in {-1.9,-0.95,0,0.95,1.9}{
 \draw[gridgreen!35,smooth,samples=14,variable=\y,domain=-1.9:1.9]
  plot({-0.85*\xx+\y},{-0.5*\xx+\H-\K*(\xx*\xx+\y*\y)});}
\draw[gray!60,smooth,samples=60,variable=\t,domain=0:360]
 plot({-1.615*cos(\t)+1.9*sin(\t)},{-0.95*cos(\t)});
% section curve at x=0: z=H-K y^2, screen x-coordinate = y
\draw[curveblue,very thick,smooth,samples=40,variable=\y,domain=-1.9:1.9]

\def\ya{0.3}\def\yb{1.2}
\fill({\ya},0)circle(1.2pt) node[below]{$(x_0,y_0)$};

\draw[->](0,0)--(2.6,0)node[right]{$y$};

\node at(1.7,3.05){$\beta$};
\node[curveblue] at(-1.5,2.9){section at fixed $x_0$};

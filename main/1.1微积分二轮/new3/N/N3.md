### 版面识别
一元情形：若 $\lim_{x\to x_0^+}f(x)\ne\lim_{x\to x_0^-}f(x)$ 则极限不存在。二元函数极限（二重极限）：$\lim_{(x,y)\to(x_0,y_0)}f(x,y)=A$ 等价于 $f(x,y)=A+\alpha$（$\alpha$ 为无穷小），用 $\varepsilon$-$\delta$ 语言表述；关键区别是 $(x,y)\to(x_0,y_0)$ 的路径有无穷多种。若有两种及以上路径使极限值不相等，则二重极限不存在；不同路径指 $y=f(x)$、$x=g(y)$ 的不同关系式，如 $(x,y)\to(0,0)$ 时沿 $y=x^3$、$x=2y$ 是两条不同路径。含一张双子图。

---

图：左：沿一条指定曲线 $y=f(x)$ 趋近 $(x_0,y_0)$；右：无穷多条缠绕路径都汇聚到 $(x_0,y_0)$，强调二元趋近路径的任意性。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\definecolor{curveblue}{rgb}{0.2,0.3,0.8}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\scriptsize]
% (a) one prescribed path y=f(x) approaching (x0,y0)
\begin{scope}
\draw[->](-0.3,0)--(3.2,0)node[right]{$x$};
\draw[->](0,-1.6)--(0,2.2)node[above]{$y$};
\fill(1.5,0.5)circle(1.3pt);\node[below left]at(1.5,0.5){$(x_0,y_0)$};
\draw[dashed,gray](1.5,0.5)--(1.5,0);\node[below]at(1.5,0){$x_0$};
\draw[dashed,gray](1.5,0.5)--(0,0.5);\node[left]at(0,0.5){$y_0$};
\draw[curveblue,thick,smooth,samples=90,domain=0.1:2.9]
  plot(\x,{0.5+0.5*sin(2.4*\x r)});
\node[curveblue]at(2.35,1.75){path $y=f(x)$};
\end{scope}
% (b) infinitely many winding paths
\begin{scope}[xshift=5.6cm]
\draw[->](-0.3,0)--(3.2,0)node[right]{$x$};
\draw[->](0,-1.6)--(0,2.2)node[above]{$y$};
\fill(1.5,0.5)circle(1.4pt);\node[below right]at(1.5,0.5){$(x_0,y_0)$};
\draw[curveblue,smooth,samples=120,variable=\t,domain=0:1]plot({1.5+1.5*\t*cos(11*\t r)},{0.5+1.5*\t*sin(11*\t r)});
\draw[gray!70,smooth,samples=120,variable=\t,domain=0:1]plot({1.5+1.4*\t*cos(11*\t r+2.1)},{0.5+1.4*\t*sin(11*\t r+2.1)});
\draw[gray!50,smooth,samples=120,variable=\t,domain=0:1]plot({1.5+1.3*\t*cos(11*\t r+4.2)},{0.5+1.3*\t*sin(11*\t r+4.2)});
\node at (1.5,-1.35){infinitely many paths to $(x_0,y_0)$};
\end{scope}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

图：钟形曲线在 $x_0$ 处达到局部极大（红点为可疑点/极值点），示意由导数变号锁定极值点。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\definecolor{curveblue}{rgb}{0.2,0.3,0.8}
\definecolor{tangentred}{rgb}{0.8,0.2,0.2}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small]
\draw[->](-0.3,0)--(3.6,0)node[right]{$x$};
\draw[->](0,-0.3)--(0,2.8)node[above]{$y$};
\draw[curveblue,thick,smooth,samples=120,domain=0.2:3.2]plot(\x,{2.1*exp(-0.55*(\x-1.5)*(\x-1.5))});
\fill[tangentred](1.5,2.1)circle(1.4pt)node[above]{suspect point (local max)};
\draw[dashed,gray](1.5,2.1)--(1.5,0);\node[below]at(1.5,0){$x_0$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

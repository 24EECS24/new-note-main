### 版面识别
例 5.11：画出 $y^2=(1-x^2)^3$ 的图像。由 $F(-x,y)=F(x,y),F(x,-y)=F(x,y),F(-x,-y)=F(x,y)$ 知关于 $y$ 轴、$x$ 轴、原点都对称，只需画第一象限 $y=(1-x^2)^{3/2}$（$x\ge0,y\ge0$，$x=1$ 时 $y=0$）再对称过去。$y'=\tfrac32\sqrt{1-x^2}(-2x)$，令 $y'=0$ 得驻点 $x=0$（$y=1$）；$y''=3(2x^2-1)/\sqrt{1-x^2}$，令 $y''=0$ 得拐点 $x=\sqrt2/2$、$y=(1/2)^{3/2}$，$(0,\sqrt2/2)$ 为凸、之后为凹。含一张闭合曲线图。

---

图：关于两轴都对称的闭合曲线 $y^2=(1-x^2)^3$，过 $(0,\pm1),(\pm1,0)$，红点标出第一象限拐点 $(\sqrt2/2,(1/2)^{3/2})$。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\definecolor{curveblue}{rgb}{0.2,0.3,0.8}
\definecolor{tangentred}{rgb}{0.8,0.2,0.2}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small]
\draw[->](-1.8,0)--(1.8,0)node[right]{$x$};
\draw[->](0,-1.7)--(0,1.9)node[above]{$y$};
% y^2=(1-x^2)^3 -> y=+-(1-x^2)^{3/2}, closed symmetric curve
\foreach \sgn in {1,-1}{
 \draw[curveblue,thick,smooth,samples=120,domain=-1:1]plot(\x,{\sgn*pow(1-\x*\x,1.5)});}
\foreach \px/\lab in {1/$1$,-1/$-1$}{\fill(\px,0)circle(1.2pt);\node[below]at(\px,0){\lab};}
\fill(0,1)circle(1.2pt);\node[above left]at(0,1){$1$};\fill(0,-1)circle(1.2pt);
\fill[tangentred]({sqrt(0.5)},{pow(0.5,1.5)})circle(1.3pt)node[above right]{$(\frac{\sqrt2}{2},(\frac12)^{3/2})$};
\node[curveblue]at(-0.2,-1.45){$y^2=(1-x^2)^3$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

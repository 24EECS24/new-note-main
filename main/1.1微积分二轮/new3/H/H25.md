### 版面识别
例 5.12：画 $y=x^x\,(x>0)$。$x\in(0,+\infty)$，$y=x^x=e^{x\ln x}$；$y'=x^x(\ln x+1)$，令为 0 得 $x=1/e$，$(0,1/e)$ 减、$(1/e,+\infty)$ 增，故 $1/e$ 是唯一极小值点；$y''=x^x(\ln x+1)^2+x^{x-1}>0$ 为凹函数。$\lim_{x\to0+}x^x=e^{\lim x\ln x}=1$、$\lim_{x\to+\infty}x^x=+\infty$。例 5.13 画 $y=e^x/x$：$y'=e^x(x-1)/x^2$，驻点 $x=1$、$x=0$ 处导数不存在。含一张 $x^x$ 图。

---

图：$y=x^x$ 在 $x\to0^+$ 趋于 1（空心点），在 $1/e$ 处取唯一极小值后凹向上递增趋 $+\infty$。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\definecolor{curveblue}{rgb}{0.2,0.3,0.8}
\definecolor{tangentred}{rgb}{0.8,0.2,0.2}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small]
\draw[->](-0.3,0)--(3.4,0)node[right]{$x$};
\draw[->](0,-0.4)--(0,2.8)node[above]{$y$};
% y=x^x = exp(x ln x), x>0; ->1 at 0+ (hole), min at 1/e
\draw[curveblue,thick,smooth,samples=140,domain=0.04:1.72]plot(\x,{exp(\x*ln(\x))});
\fill[white](0,1)circle(1.7pt);\draw(0,1)circle(1.7pt);\node[left]at(0,1){$1$};
\fill[tangentred]({1/2.71828},{exp((1/2.71828)*ln(1/2.71828))})circle(1.4pt);
\draw[dashed,gray]({1/2.71828},0)--({1/2.71828},{exp((1/2.71828)*ln(1/2.71828))});
\node[below]at({1/2.71828},0){$1/e$};
\node[curveblue]at(2.2,2.3){$y=x^x\,(x>0)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

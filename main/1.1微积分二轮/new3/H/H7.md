### 版面识别
三、凹凸性与拐点的概念·凹凸性定义。定义 1：$f$ 在区间 $I$ 连续，对任意不同两点 $x_1,x_2$，恒有 $f((x_1+x_2)/2)<[f(x_1)+f(x_2)]/2$ 则图形是凹的（凹向上，弦在曲线上方），$>$ 则是凸的。更一般地写为 $f(ax_1+bx_2)\lessgtr af(x_1)+bf(x_2)$，其中 $0<a,b<1,a+b=1$。定义 2：$f$ 在 $[a,b]$ 连续、$(a,b)$ 可导，若 $f(x_0)+f'(x_0)(x-x_0)\lessgtr f(x)$（切线在曲线下方/上方）则为凹/凸。含一张凹凸弦图。

---

图：左（凹）：凹向上曲线，连接 $x_1,x_2$ 的弦（红）在曲线上方，中点处 $f(mid)<$ 弦中点；右（凸）：拱型曲线，弦在曲线下方，$f(mid)>$ 弦中点。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\definecolor{curveblue}{rgb}{0.2,0.3,0.8}
\definecolor{tangentred}{rgb}{0.8,0.2,0.2}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\scriptsize]
% concave up: chord above curve
\begin{scope}
\draw[->](-0.3,0)--(4,0)node[right]{$x$};\draw[->](0,-0.3)--(0,3)node[above]{$y$};
\def\xA{0.9}\def\xB{3.1}
\draw[curveblue,thick,smooth,samples=90,domain=0.4:3.6]plot(\x,{0.42*(\x-2)*(\x-2)+0.5});
\fill(\xA,{0.42*(\xA-2)^2+0.5})circle(1.2pt);\fill(\xB,{0.42*(\xB-2)^2+0.5})circle(1.2pt);
\draw[tangentred,thick](\xA,{0.42*(\xA-2)^2+0.5})--(\xB,{0.42*(\xB-2)^2+0.5});
\foreach \xx in {\xA,2,\xB}{\draw[dashed](\xx,0)--(\xx,{0.42*(\xx-2)^2+0.5+0.02});}
\node[below]at(\xA,0){$x_1$};\node[below]at(2,0){$\frac{x_1+x_2}{2}$};\node[below]at(\xB,0){$x_2$};
\node at(2,-0.85){concave up: chord above, $f(mid)<\frac{f(x_1)+f(x_2)}2$};\end{scope}
% concave down: chord below curve
\begin{scope}[xshift=6.2cm]
\draw[->](-0.3,0)--(4,0)node[right]{$x$};\draw[->](0,-0.3)--(0,3)node[above]{$y$};
\def\uA{0.9}\def\uB{3.1}
\draw[curveblue,thick,smooth,samples=90,domain=0.4:3.6]plot(\x,{2.3-0.42*(\x-2)*(\x-2)});
\fill(\uA,{2.3-0.42*(\uA-2)^2})circle(1.2pt);\fill(\uB,{2.3-0.42*(\uB-2)^2})circle(1.2pt);
\draw[tangentred,thick](\uA,{2.3-0.42*(\uA-2)^2})--(\uB,{2.3-0.42*(\uB-2)^2});
\foreach \xx in {\uA,2,\uB}{\draw[dashed](\xx,0)--(\xx,{2.3-0.42*(\xx-2)^2});}
\node[below]at(\uA,0){$x_1$};\node[below]at(2,0){$\frac{x_1+x_2}{2}$};\node[below]at(\uB,0){$x_2$};
\node at(2,-0.85){concave down: chord below, $f(mid)>\frac{f(x_1)+f(x_2)}2$};\end{scope}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

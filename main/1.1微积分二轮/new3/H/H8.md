### 版面识别
几何意义：曲线在 $x=x_0$ 处切线方程 $y-y_0=k(x-x_0)$，即 $y=f(x_0)+f'(x_0)(x-x_0)$；若曲线在任一点处的切线（除该点外）总在曲线下方（上方），则曲线是凹（凸）的。拐点定义：连续曲线凹弧与凸弧的分界点，拐点处只需连续、凹凸不分先后、拐点在曲线上写 $(x_0,f(x_0))$。判别：$I$ 上二阶可导，$f''(x)>0$ 为凹、$f''(x)<0$ 为凸；理解记忆：$f''>0$ 则 $f'$ 单调增、斜率不断增大，字形形成凹。含一张切线凹凸图。

图：左（凹）：切线（红）除切点外总在曲线下方；右（凸）：切线总在曲线上方；区间标 $a,x_0,b$。

\begin{document}
\definecolor{curveblue}{rgb}{0.2,0.3,0.8}
\definecolor{tangentred}{rgb}{0.8,0.2,0.2}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\scriptsize]
% concave up: tangent below curve
\begin{scope}
\draw[->](-0.3,0)--(4,0)node[right]{$x$};\draw[->](0,-0.4)--(0,2.8)node[above]{$y$};
\draw[curveblue,thick,smooth,samples=90,domain=0.4:3.6]plot(\x,{0.4*(\x-2)*(\x-2)+0.7});
\draw[tangentred,thick](0.55,{0.7-0.0})--(3.5,{0.7+1.2*1.5});
\node[below]at(2,0){$x_0$};\node[below]at(0.7,0){$a$};\node[below]at(3.3,0){$b$};
\node at(2,-0.8){concave up: tangent lies below curve};\end{scope}
% concave down: tangent above curve
\begin{scope}[xshift=6.2cm]
\draw[->](-0.3,0)--(4,0)node[right]{$x$};\draw[->](0,-0.4)--(0,2.8)node[above]{$y$};
\draw[curveblue,thick,smooth,samples=90,domain=0.4:3.6]plot(\x,{2.1-0.4*(\x-2)*(\x-2)});
\draw[tangentred,thick](0.55,{2.1-1.2*1.5})--(3.5,{2.1+0.0});
\node[below]at(2,0){$x_0$};\node[below]at(0.7,0){$a$};\node[below]at(3.3,0){$b$};
\node at(2,-0.8){concave down: tangent lies above curve};\end{scope}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}

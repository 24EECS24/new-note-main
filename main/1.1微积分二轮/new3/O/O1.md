### 版面识别
多元函数微分法则·链式求导规则。难点是对“标号（下标）”的辨别与理解。设 $z=f(u,v),u=\varphi(x,y),v=w(x,y)$，即 $z=f[\varphi(x,y),w(x,y)]$，给出 $\partial z/\partial x=z_u u_x+z_v v_x$、$\partial z/\partial y=z_u u_y+z_v v_y$；注 (1) 当 $u=\varphi(t),v=w(t)$ 时为全导 $dz/dt=z_u du/dt+z_v dv/dt$；注 (2) 无论对哪个变量求导、求几阶导，新函数仍保持与原函数相同的复合结构。compare 一元 $y=f(u),u=\varphi(x)$，$dy/dx=(dy/du)(du/dx)$。含两张变量依赖结构图。

---

图1：二元复合 $z=f(u,v),u=\varphi(x,y),v=w(x,y)$ 的变量依赖：$z$ 依赖 $u,v$，$u,v$ 又各自依赖 $x,y$（全连接），对应两条偏导求和路径。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small,
  v/.style={circle,draw,minimum size=7mm,inner sep=1pt},
  every edge/.style={draw,-stealth}]
\node[v](z) at (0,2.2){$z$};
\node[v](u) at (-1.3,1.1){$u$};
\node[v](v) at (1.3,1.1){$v$};
\node[v](x) at (-1.7,0){$x$};
\node[v](y) at (1.7,0){$y$};
\path (z)edge(u)(z)edge(v);
\path (u)edge(x)(u)edge(y)(v)edge(x)(v)edge(y);
\node[left] at (z.west){$z=f(u,v)$};
\node[left] at (u.west){$u=\varphi(x,y)$};
\node[right] at (v.east){$v=w(x,y)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

图2：一元 compare：$y=f(u),u=\varphi(x)$ 是单链 $y\to u\to x$，对应 $dy/dx=(dy/du)(du/dx)$。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small,v/.style={circle,draw,minimum size=7mm}]
\node[v](y) at (0,2){$y$};
\node[v](u) at (0,1){$u$};
\node[v](x) at (0,0){$x$};
\draw[-stealth](y)--(u);\draw[-stealth](u)--(x);
\node[right] at (y.east){$y=f(u)$};\node[right] at (u.east){$u=\varphi(x)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

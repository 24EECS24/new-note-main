### 版面识别
隐函数偏导的变式：当把 $z$（或 $y$）视为由方程确定的因变量时，$F\to(x,y,z)\to(x,y)$ 的依赖中因变量对各自变量的偏导由 $-F_{(\cdot)}'/F_{因变量}'$ 给出；练习辨别“谁是因变量、对谁求导”。含一张依赖结构图。

---

图1：$F\to(x,y,z)\to(x,y)$ 依赖图，帮助辨认因变量 $z$ 与自变量 $x,y$ 之间的路径。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small,v/.style={circle,draw,minimum size=6.5mm,inner sep=1pt}]
\node[v](F) at (0,2.4){$F$};
\node[v](a) at (-2,1.2){$x$};
\node[v](b) at (0,1.2){$y$};
\node[v](c) at (2,1.2){$z$};
\node[v](x) at (-0.9,0){$x$};
\node[v](y) at (0.9,0){$y$};
\draw[-stealth](F)--(a);\draw[-stealth](F)--(b);\draw[-stealth](F)--(c);
\draw[-stealth](a)--(x);\draw[-stealth](b)--(y);\draw[-stealth](c)--(x);\draw[-stealth](c)--(y);
\node[left] at (F.west){$F=F(x,y,z)$};\node[right] at (c.east){$z=z(x,y)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

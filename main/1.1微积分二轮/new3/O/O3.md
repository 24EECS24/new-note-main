### 版面识别
例 13.9：$z=u^2v-uv^2$，$u=x\cos y,v=x\sin y$，求 $\partial z/\partial x,\partial z/\partial y$。先用链式法则写出 $z_x=z_u u_x+z_v v_x$、$z_y=z_u u_y+z_v v_y$，再代入 $u,v$ 化简（结果含 $x^2$ 与三角式）。含一张变量依赖结构图。

---

图1：$z\to(u,v)\to(x,y)$ 的依赖图：$z$ 对 $x$（或 $y$）的偏导等于经 $u$、经 $v$ 两条路径之和。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small,
  v/.style={circle,draw,minimum size=7mm,inner sep=1pt},
  every edge/.style={draw,-stealth}]
\node[v](z) at (0,2.2){$z$};
\node[v](u) at (-1.3,1.1){$u$};
\node[v](v) at (1.3,1.1){$v$};
\node[v](x) at (-1.7,0){$x$};
\node[v](y) at (1.7,0){$y$};
\path (z)edge(u)(z)edge(v);
\path (u)edge(x)(u)edge(y)(v)edge(x)(v)edge(y);
\node[left] at (z.west){$z=f(u,v)$};
\node[left] at (u.west){$u=\varphi(x,y)$};
\node[right] at (v.east){$v=w(x,y)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

例 13.15：两层复合函数的全微分。第一层 $F=F(x,y,z)$，第二层 $z=z(x,y)$，比普通复合多了一层；用形式不变性逐层微分再回代。含一张两层依赖结构图。

---

图1：两层复合 $F\to(x,y,z)\to(x,y)$：比一元复合多一层，$z$ 不是自变量而是 $x,y$ 的函数。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small,v/.style={circle,draw,minimum size=6.5mm,inner sep=1pt}]
\node[v](F) at (0,2.4){$F$};
\node[v](a) at (-2,1.2){$x$};
\node[v](b) at (0,1.2){$y$};
\node[v](c) at (2,1.2){$z$};
\node[v](x) at (-0.9,0){$x$};
\node[v](y) at (0.9,0){$y$};
\draw[-stealth](F)--(a);\draw[-stealth](F)--(b);\draw[-stealth](F)--(c);
\draw[-stealth](a)--(x);\draw[-stealth](b)--(y);\draw[-stealth](c)--(x);\draw[-stealth](c)--(y);
\node[left] at (F.west){$F=F(x,y,z)$};\node[right] at (c.east){$z=z(x,y)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

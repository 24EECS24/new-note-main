### 版面识别
链式法则的三种复合情形归纳：(一) $z=f(u,v),u=\varphi(x,y),v=w(x,y)$（两中间变量、两自变量）；(二) $z=f(u,v),u=\varphi(t),v=w(t)$（两中间变量、一自变量，结果为全导 $dz/dt$）；(三) compare 一元单链。强调“几条路径就几项、每段路径相乘、分叉相加”。含两张结构图。

---

图1：情形一：两中间变量 $u,v$ 到两自变量 $x,y$ 的全连接依赖图。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small,
  v/.style={circle,draw,minimum size=7mm,inner sep=1pt},
  every edge/.style={draw,-stealth}]
\node[v](z) at (0,2.2){$z$};
\node[v](u) at (-1.3,1.1){$u$};
\node[v](v) at (1.3,1.1){$v$};
\node[v](x) at (-1.7,0){$x$};
\node[v](y) at (1.7,0){$y$};
\path (z)edge(u)(z)edge(v);
\path (u)edge(x)(u)edge(y)(v)edge(x)(v)edge(y);
\node[left] at (z.west){$z=f(u,v)$};
\node[left] at (u.west){$u=\varphi(x,y)$};
\node[right] at (v.east){$v=w(x,y)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

图2：情形二：两中间变量 $u=\varphi(t),v=w(t)$ 收敛到同一自变量 $t$，对应全导 $dz/dt=z_u u'+z_v v'$。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small,v/.style={circle,draw,minimum size=7mm}]
\node[v](z) at (0,2.2){$z$};
\node[v](u) at (-1.2,1.1){$u$};
\node[v](v) at (1.2,1.1){$v$};
\node[v](t) at (0,0){$t$};
\draw[-stealth](z)--(u);\draw[-stealth](z)--(v);
\draw[-stealth](u)--(t);\draw[-stealth](v)--(t);
\node[left] at (u.west){$u=\varphi(t)$};\node[right] at (v.east){$v=w(t)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

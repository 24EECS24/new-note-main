### 版面识别
分析例 13.15 为两层复合（$F=F(x,y,z),z=z(x,y)$）；例 13.16 设 $z=z(x,y)$ 由 $F(x+2y^{-1},y+2x^{-1})=0$ 确定、$F$ 有连续偏导且 $xF_1'+yF_2'\neq0$，求 $x z_x+y z_y$。例 13.16 是三层复合：$F\to(\text{位置1,位置2})\to(x,y,z)\to(x,y)$。含两张结构图。

图2：例 13.16 的三层结构：$F$ 先依赖两个位置表达式（$x+z/y$、$y+z/x$），它们再依赖 $x,y,z$，而 $z=z(x,y)$，对应更复杂的链式回路。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small,v/.style={circle,draw,minimum size=6.5mm,inner sep=1pt}]
\node[v](F) at (0,3.3){$F$};
\node[v](u) at (-1.1,2.2){$u$};
\node[v](v) at (1.1,2.2){$v$};
\node[v](x3) at (-2.2,1.1){$x$};
\node[v](y3) at (0,1.1){$y$};
\node[v](z3) at (2.2,1.1){$z$};
\node[v](x) at (-1,0){$x$};
\node[v](y) at (1,0){$y$};
\draw[-stealth](F)--(u);\draw[-stealth](F)--(v);
\foreach \a in {u,v}{\foreach \b in {x3,y3,z3}{\draw[-stealth](\a)--(\b);}}
\draw[-stealth](x3)--(x);\draw[-stealth](y3)--(y);\draw[-stealth](z3)--(x);\draw[-stealth](z3)--(y);
\node[left] at (F.west){$F=F(u,v)$};
\node[left] at (u.west){$u=u(x,y,z)$};\node[right] at (v.east){$v=v(x,y,z)$};
\node[right] at (z3.east){$z=z(x,y)$};\node[right] at (y3.east){$y=y(x)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

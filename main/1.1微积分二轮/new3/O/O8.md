### 版面识别
把链式法则用于“只有一个自变量但中间变量有多个”的全导情形：一元单链 $y\to u\to x$；以及 $z=f(u,v)$、$u=u(y),v=v(y)$ 时 $dz/dy=z_u u'+z_v v'$（两条路径在 $y$ 处收敛）。含两张结构图。

---

图1：一元单链 $y\to u\to x$。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small,v/.style={circle,draw,minimum size=7mm}]
\node[v](y) at (0,2){$y$};
\node[v](u) at (0,1){$u$};
\node[v](x) at (0,0){$x$};
\draw[-stealth](y)--(u);\draw[-stealth](u)--(x);
\node[right] at (y.east){$y=f(u)$};\node[right] at (u.east){$u=\varphi(x)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

图2：两中间变量 $u,v$ 同时依赖同一自变量 $y$，全导为两路径之和。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small,v/.style={circle,draw,minimum size=7mm}]
\node[v](z) at (0,2.2){$z$};
\node[v](u) at (-1.2,1.1){$u$};
\node[v](v) at (1.2,1.1){$v$};
\node[v](t) at (0,0){$t$};
\draw[-stealth](z)--(u);\draw[-stealth](z)--(v);
\draw[-stealth](u)--(t);\draw[-stealth](v)--(t);
\node[left] at (u.west){$u=\varphi(t)$};\node[right] at (v.east){$v=w(t)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

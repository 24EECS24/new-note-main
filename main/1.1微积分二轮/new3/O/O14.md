### 版面识别
三元方程 $F(x,y,z)=0$ 确定二元隐函数 $z=z(x,y)$：当 $F_z'\neq0$ 时 $\partial z/\partial x=-F_x'/F_z'$、$\partial z/\partial y=-F_y'/F_z'$。结构图显示 $F$ 依赖 $x,y,z$，而 $z$ 又依赖 $x,y$。含一张依赖结构图。

---

图1：两层结构 $F=F(x,y,z),z=z(x,y)$：$x,y$ 直达，$z$ 又连回 $x,y$，对应隐函数偏导公式中的回路修正。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,font=\small,v/.style={circle,draw,minimum size=6.5mm,inner sep=1pt}]
\node[v](F) at (0,2.4){$F$};
\node[v](a) at (-2,1.2){$x$};
\node[v](b) at (0,1.2){$y$};
\node[v](c) at (2,1.2){$z$};
\node[v](x) at (-0.9,0){$x$};
\node[v](y) at (0.9,0){$y$};
\draw[-stealth](F)--(a);\draw[-stealth](F)--(b);\draw[-stealth](F)--(c);
\draw[-stealth](a)--(x);\draw[-stealth](b)--(y);\draw[-stealth](c)--(x);\draw[-stealth](c)--(y);
\node[left] at (F.west){$F=F(x,y,z)$};\node[right] at (c.east){$z=z(x,y)$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

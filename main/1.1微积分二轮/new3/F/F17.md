### 版面识别
先说明导函数自身在一点的连续性：$\lim_{x\to x_0}f'(x)=f'(x_0)$ 即 $f'$ 在 $x_0$ 连续，并与前述函数连续、各阶可导的层级关系照应。例 3.8：设 $f$ 在 $x_0$ 二阶可导且 $f'(x_0)=0,f''(x_0)\neq0$，用二阶导数定义与局部保号性判断 $f'(x)$ 在 $x_0$ 两侧变号——$f''(x_0)<0$ 时左侧 $f'>0$、右侧 $f'<0$，$f$ 在 $x_0$ 取极大值；$f''(x_0)>0$ 取极小值。本页含 1 张导数变号数轴图。

---

图1：以 $x_0$ 为中心的数轴，标出 $x_0-\delta,x_0,x_0+\delta$，左段标 $f'(x)>0$（递增）、右段标 $f'(x)<0$（递减），说明 $x_0$ 为极大值点。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\definecolor{gridgreen}{rgb}{0,0.39,0}
\definecolor{tangentred}{rgb}{0.8,0.2,0.2}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,scale=1.4,font=\small]
\draw[->] (-2.3,0)--(2.3,0) node[right]{$x$};
\foreach \px/\lbl in {-1.4/x_0-\delta,0/x_0,1.4/x_0+\delta}{
  \draw (\px,0.08)--(\px,-0.08);
  \node[below] at (\px,0){$\lbl$};
}
\fill (0,0) circle (1.3pt);
% sign of f'(x) drawn on the axis
\draw[gridgreen,line width=1pt] (-1.4,0.12)--(0,0.12);
\draw[tangentred,line width=1pt] (0,0.12)--(1.4,0.12);
\node[gridgreen] at (-0.7,0.5){$f'(x)>0$};
\node[tangentred] at (0.7,0.5){$f'(x)<0$};
% interval brackets (plain lines, no decoration library)
\draw (-1.4,0.82)--(-1.4,0.7); \draw (0,0.82)--(0,0.7); \draw (-1.4,0.82)--(0,0.82);
\draw (0,-0.72)--(0,-0.84); \draw (1.4,-0.72)--(1.4,-0.84); \draw (0,-0.84)--(1.4,-0.84);
\node[font=\scriptsize] at (-0.7,1.05){increasing};
\node[font=\scriptsize] at (0.7,-1.08){decreasing};
\node at (0,1.4){local maximum at $x_0$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

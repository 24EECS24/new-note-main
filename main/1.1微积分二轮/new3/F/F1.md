### 版面识别
一元函数微分学开篇·导数引例。左栏依次给出区间 $[t_0,t_1]$、$[t_2,t_3]$、$[t_0,t_3]$ 上的平均变化率 $\frac{f(t+\Delta t)-f(t)}{\Delta t}$，并指出整段 $[t_0,t_3]$ 的平均变化率为 0、会掩盖中间过程，说明平均变化率是粗糙概念；右栏通过令 $\Delta t\to 0$ 引入瞬时变化率即导数，给出拉格朗日记号 $f'(t)$、莱布尼茨记号 $\frac{df(t)}{dt}$、牛顿记号 $\dot f(t)$。本页共 2 张示意图。

---

图1：位移函数 $x=f(t)$ 的“上升—水平平台—光滑回落”分段曲线，横轴标出 $t_0,t_1,t_2,t_3$，直观说明整段平均变化率为 0 但中间过程并不为 0。

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,scale=1.1,font=\small]
% ---- fig 1: piecewise x=f(t): rise, flat top, smooth fall ----
\begin{scope}
\draw[->] (-0.2,0)--(3.7,0) node[right]{$t$};
\draw[->] (0,-0.2)--(0,1.65) node[above]{$x=f(t)$};
\draw[thick] (0,0)--(1.2,1.2);
\draw[thick] (1.2,1.2)--(2.0,1.2);
\draw[thick] (2.0,1.2) .. controls (2.35,1.12) and (2.75,0.12) .. (3.2,0);
\draw[dashed,gray] (1.2,0)--(1.2,1.2);
\draw[dashed,gray] (2.0,0)--(2.0,1.2);
\foreach \px/\lbl in {0/t_0,1.2/t_1,2.0/t_2,3.2/t_3}
  \node[below] at (\px,0){$\lbl$};
\node at (1.6,-0.75){average rate on $[t_0,t_3]$ is $0$};
\end{scope}
% ---- fig 2: decreasing convex curve with t and t+dt ----
\begin{scope}[xshift=5.4cm]
\draw[->] (-0.2,0)--(2.7,0) node[right]{$t$};
\draw[->] (0,-0.2)--(0,1.65) node[above]{$x=f(t)$};
\draw[thick,smooth,samples=100,domain=0.1:2.3] plot(\x,{1.7*exp(-1.1*\x)+0.15});
\draw[dashed,gray] (0.85,0)--(0.85,0.817);
\draw[dashed,gray] (1.45,0)--(1.45,0.495);
\node[below] at (0.85,-0.02){$t$};
\node[below] at (1.45,-0.02){$t+\Delta t$};
\end{scope}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

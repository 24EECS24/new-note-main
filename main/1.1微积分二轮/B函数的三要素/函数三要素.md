# 基本初等函数的定义域

| 函数                      | 定义域               |
| ----------------------- | ----------------- |
| $\ln x$                 | $x>0$             |
| $\sqrt{x}$              | $x\ge 0$          |
| $\dfrac{1}{x}$          | $x\ne 0$          |
| $x^{0}$                 | $x\ne 0$          |
| $sinx$                  | $x\in \mathbb{R}$ |
| $\cos x$                | $x\in \mathbb{R}$ |
| $\tan x$                | $x\ne 0$          |
| $\dfrac{1}{\ln x}$      | $x>0$ 且 $x\ne 1$  |
| $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}$   | $x>0$             |
| $\ln\ln x$              | $x>1$             |
| $\dfrac{1}{\ln\ln x}$   | $x>1$ 且 $x\ne e$  |
| $\arcsin x$、$\arccos x$ | $x\in[-1,1]$      |
| $\arctan x$             | $x\in \mathbb{R}$ |

# 复合函数
## 二层复合
### 定义域与值域

$$
\begin{align*}
若\quad &D_{a} \to a(\ )\to R_{a}\quad(\text{第一层：内层})\\[4pt]
&D_{b} \to b(\ )\to R_{b}\quad(\text{第二层：外层})\\[4pt]
&D \to b\big[a(\ )\big]\to R\\[4pt]
则\quad &  \\[4pt]
&\left.\begin{matrix}
 D_{b}\xrightarrow[]{a^{-1}(\ )} \Box 
\\[4pt]
D_{a}\cap \Box =D
\end{matrix}\right\} D=D_{a}\cap a^{-1}(D_{b}) \\[4pt]
&\left.\begin{matrix}
 D\xrightarrow[]{a(\ )}R_{a}\cap D_{b}\xrightarrow[]{b(\ )}R
\\[4pt]
D_{a}\xrightarrow[]{a(\ )}R_{a}，R_{a}\cap D_{b}\xrightarrow[]{b(\ )}R
\end{matrix}\right\} R=b\big[a(D)\big]=b\big[R_{a}\cap D_{b}\big] \\[4pt]
\end{align*}
$$

`````col
&D_{a}与D_{b}往往都能直接得到\\[4pt]
\end{align*}
`````

## 三层复合同理

### 定义域与值域
$$
\begin{align*}
若\quad &D_{a} \to a(\ )\to R_{a}\quad(\text{第一层：最内层})\\[4pt]
&D_{b} \to b(\ )\to R_{b}\quad(\text{第二层：中间层})\\[4pt]
&D_{c} \to c(\ )\to R_{c}\quad(\text{第三层：最外层})\\[4pt]
&D \to c\big[b\big[a(\ )\big]\big]\to R\\[4pt]
则\quad &  \\[4pt]
&\left.\begin{matrix}
 D_{c}\xrightarrow[]{b^{-1}(\ )} \Box_1 
\\[4pt]
\Box_1\xrightarrow[]{a^{-1}(\ )} \Box_2
\\[4pt]
D_{a}\cap \Box_2 =D
\end{matrix}\right\} D=D_{a}\cap a^{-1}\big(b^{-1}(D_{c})\big) \\[8pt]
&\left.\begin{matrix}
 D\xrightarrow[]{a(\ )}R_{a}\cap b^{-1}(D_c)\xrightarrow[]{b(\ )}R_{b}\cap D_{c}\xrightarrow[]{c(\ )}R
\\[4pt]
D_{a}\xrightarrow[]{a(\ )}R_{a}，R_{a}\cap D_b\xrightarrow[]{b(\ )}R_b'，R_b'\cap D_c\xrightarrow[]{c(\ )}R
\end{matrix}\right\} R=c\Big[b\big[a(D)\big]\Big]=c\Big[b\big[R_a\cap D_b\big]\cap D_c\Big] \\[4pt]
\end{align*}
$$
### 对应关系

$$
\begin{align*}
&对于复合函数w(\ )=c\big[b\big[a(\ )\big]\big]\\[4pt]
&给出w(\ )、a(\ )、b(\ )，求c(\ )\\[4pt]
\end{align*}
$$

`````col
````col-md
flexGrow=1
===
==法1 反函数剥离法==
$$
\begin{align*}
&若a、b均为一一对应函数，存在单值反函数\\[4pt]
&连续两次反函数代入，逐层向内剥离\\[4pt]
&先剥离第二层b，再剥离第一层a\\[4pt]
&本质：c\big(b\big(a\big(a^{-1}\big(b^{-1}(t)\big)\big)\big)\big) = c(t)\\[4pt]
\end{align*}
$$
````
````col-md
flexGrow=1
===
$$
\begin{align*}
&由b(\ )求b^{-1}(\ )，由a(\ )求a^{-1}(\ )\\[4pt]
&c(\ )=w\big[a^{-1}\big[b^{-1}(\ )\big]\big] \\[4pt]
&= c\left[ b\left[ a\left[ a^{-1}\left[ b^{-1}(\ ) \right] \right] \right] \right] \\[4pt]
\end{align*}
$$
````
`````

`````col
````col-md

&利用恒等变形，逐层构造内层整体\\[4pt]
&先在w(\ )中凑出b(\ )的整体形式\\[4pt]
&再进一步凑出a(\ )的整体形式\\[4pt]
&\Rightarrow c(\ ) \\[4pt]
\end{align*}
$$

&将w(\ )恒等变形，逐层匹配内层结构\\[4pt]
&使w(\ )=c\big[b\big[a(\ )\big]\big]\\[4pt]
&直接还原最外层c(\ )的对应法则 \\[4pt]
\end{align*}
$$
`````
